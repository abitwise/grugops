3. THE EIGHT-FIXTURE SWEEP, COMPARED BLOCK BY BLOCK
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The post-extraction sweep was captured with the identical loop, against the REBUILT
`scripts/validate-agent-factory.js`, and diffed against the sixteen blocks recorded in section 3 of
32-08-RED-baseline.txt:

    diff <pre-extraction sweep> <post-extraction sweep>
    -> no output
    SWEEP IDENTICAL: 16/16 blocks, 0 differences

EVERY DIFFERENCE IS CLASSIFIED, AND THERE ARE NONE TO CLASSIFY.

The plan required each difference between the two sweeps to be recorded as one of the two named
deviations or as a regression, with no third category. The difference set is EMPTY, so the
classification is vacuous — and a vacuous classification is exactly the thing worth stating out loud
rather than reporting as a clean pass.

WHY IT IS EMPTY, MEASURED RATHER THAN GUESSED.

All eight fixture boards carry the same four headings:

    ## Ready (WIP 0/8)
    ## In Development (WIP 0/3)
    ## In Review (WIP 0/3)
    ## Done (WIP unlimited)

Each is `limited` or `unlimited` under D-05, legal under the old strip AND under this grammar, and
normalizes identically under both. No fixture board carries a Blocked heading, and none carries a
`##` line whose suffix is non-canonical. So neither named deviation is REACHABLE through this sweep.

That makes the sweep a regression instrument and not a deviation instrument. It answers "did the
extraction change any verdict it was not supposed to change" with sixteen identical blocks. It
cannot answer "did the two intended deviations happen" — the two behavioural cases in section 2
answer that, on boards written to carry the two headings the fixtures lack. Reading the
zero-difference sweep as evidence the deviations did not occur would invert what it measures.

# Plan 29-07 style dispositions

The last two role files — `agent-factory/roles/security-nfr.md` and
`agent-factory/roles/compliance-officer.md` — rewritten onto the canonical role skeleton, following
the rules plans 29-05 and 29-06 proved: `## One job` owns the what as one sentence, `## Caveman
prompt` owns identity and attitude, `## Responsibilities` owns the how, and `## Hard limits` is the
only place a prohibition appears (D-19). The caveman block is the section that gives up its content
(D-09), the second-person copula opener is rewritten rather than exempted (D-41), and each role
gains one when-absent config fallback sentence in `## Reads` (D-30) — added only after that role's
removals landed and were measured (D-37).

**These two files are different from the fifteen that came before them, and the difference is the
reason they were planned apart.** Each carries a fenced caveman block *and* clear-voice safety or
compliance text in one document. The project's voice discipline (`CLAUDE.md` § Constraints) requires
the safety half stay in clear voice: *the joke earns trust, it never replaces the explanation or
muddies a safety topic.* So this plan does not begin with an edit. It begins with a written
**three-way partition** of each file's sentences — safety-bearing clear voice, style-only prose, and
the caveman block — so that a later reader can see which sentences were **judged** load-bearing
rather than infer it from what happened to change.

Both files are on the `docs/audit/28-safety-surface-exclusions.md` list, as is every other role
file. D-02's rule bites hardest here: safety sections are **not** exempt from the profile. They
receive it deliberately, one file at a time, each change dispositioned. Freezing them would leave
the kit's most safety-critical prose as its least controlled text, which inverts LANG-02 at exactly
the point where two agents most need to reach the same act.

**No safety rule was compressed to make a byte target.** Where a rule could not survive telegraphic
compression it was **deleted from the compressed position** and left standing, byte-unchanged, in
the clear-voice section that owns it — the precedent 29-06 set twice. `security-nfr.md` has the
tightest budget in the phase, and the temptation to buy bytes with a safety sentence was therefore
strongest here; the four removals below are the only ones taken, and each is named.

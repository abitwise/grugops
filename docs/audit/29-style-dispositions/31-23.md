## Who wrote this file, and why that is stated first

**Plan `31-23` did not write it. Plan `31-30` did**, on 2026-09-10, in gap-closure round 6 wave 4.

`docs/audit/31-round5-residuals.md` §6.3 measured round 5's own plans adding **35** findings to a gate
that was already red, and recorded the remedy as one disposition file per owning plan — while
declining to write them, on the standing argument that "writing rows for another plan's clauses puts a
`before`/`after` and a reason in the register that this plan did not make and cannot vouch for". That
argument is correct about SUBSTANCE and it is the reason this file's rows are written the way they
are: **every row's attribution is DERIVED, not assumed**, and every row records what the clause SAYS
rather than a reason its author never gave.

**The derivation.** `attributeClauses()` — the gate's own attribution, the same one
`companionSatisfied` consults — was run over the watched corpus at commit `8cff6d8`, and each
undispositioned clause was mapped to the carrier commits that changed it. The six rows below are
exactly the clauses whose carrier set is `a38be64`
(`fix(31-23): the bound bounds ASCENT, not inspection — home answers only as a repository (CR-13, D-26)`)
**and no other carrier**. No clause with a shared or absent carrier appears here; each of those is
recorded under "What is NOT in this file" below, with its owner.

## What the change was

`CR-13` of `31-REVIEW.md`, reproduced by `31-VERIFICATION.md` round 5: `31-19`'s home-directory bound
answered "may the walk climb here?" and "may the walk look here?" with ONE predicate, asked BEFORE the
directory was inspected. A repository whose root IS the home directory therefore had its own `.git`
and its own `.grugops/factory.config.json` skipped entirely, and the answer fell through to the kit's
lean default — a configuration moving from refused to admitted, which is the `WR-15` direction.

`D-26` split the predicate: the bound bounds ASCENT, and home is inspected exactly once, answering
only as a repository. The workflow sentences below are that mechanism stated for a reader. They are
rewritten rather than softened, and each is short because `WP-02` bounds a procedural sentence at 20
words and `WP-03` a descriptive one at 25.

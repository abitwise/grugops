Every clause plan `31-29` changes in the watched corpus is recorded here, one row per clause, per the

## Why this file changed

Plan `31-29` closes `CR-19` and `CR-20` of `31-REVIEW.md`, both independently reproduced by
`31-VERIFICATION.md` round 6 against the committed `scripts/context-io.js`, and lands decision
**D-31**: a bound is owned by the side that admits, and two halves of one action are keyed on one
variable.

`agent-factory/workflows/18-context-compaction.md` is edited for one reason and one reason only: two
sentences it already carried were measured FALSE of the mechanism, and one number in it was measured
wrong. None of the three is softened to match a defect.

**The ledger paragraph (line 83).** Round 5 rewrote this paragraph to assert that "a re-binding first
looks in the destination repository's ledger" and that "the destination never holds a human-disposed
finding with no ledger line". Round 6 measured both false with three real governance roots: the note
went to the caller's `to` and the GOV-02 event went to `repoRoot`, so a human-disposed finding landed
in one repository's store while its audit record landed in another's ledger. The MECHANISM is what
changed — the owning repository is now derived from the destination store through one authority, and
both halves key on that answer — and the sentences are rewritten to describe the mechanism that now
exists. A sentence is never left standing when the code disagrees with it, and it is never weakened
to make the disagreement go away.

**The forgery price (line 75).** `WR-28` measured the stop condition's "three filesystem operations
inside this repository" against a repository carrying a version-control marker and NO governance
configuration, and found TWO. The marker is not load-bearing there, because the enclosing boundary
carries no configuration and the walk answers `nearest` with the forged marker absent. The variable
is the ENCLOSING repository's configuration, not the fact of being inside one, so the price is now
stated per position — three positions, three numbers, all three driven in `scripts/context-io.test.ts`
and all three stated identically in `T-31-18-01`, in `governanceRootOf`'s docstring and here.

**Sentence length.** The rewritten sentences are short because the Phase 29 writing profile bounds a
procedural sentence at 20 words and a descriptive one at 25. The prose was split rather than the
corpus narrowed.

**Scope note.** The rows below cover the clauses this plan AUTHORED. The remaining clauses reported
at lines 56 and 83 are pre-existing prose whose diff position moved because this plan rewrote
sentences in the same paragraph; they are dispositioned by the plans that wrote them and are not
re-claimed here.

# Plan 39-18 style dispositions (gap closure round 3; WR-01)

Plan 39-18 is the second plan of the third Phase 39 gap closure round. It settles advisory WR-01
(TIER-04, D-19..D-21) from the Phase 39 re-verification. It edits two watched documents:
`agent-factory/workflows/04-ticket-to-pr.md` (step 15 and the used-up bullet in `## Stop
conditions`) and `agent-factory/roles/tech-lead.md` (`## Hard limits`). It also edits
`agent-factory/config/factory.config.md`, which the gate does not watch.

- **WR-01** (TIER-04, D-19..D-21): the used-up stop is tagged `checkpoint:
  escalate_unadjudicable_result`, and step 15 says it follows the same value as the re-score stop.
  The text defined `notify` and `off` only for the re-score case. At a lowered cell a used-up ticket
  had no legal move and no `Move:` label for a kept level, while the stop condition and tech-lead.md
  still read as an unconditional named-human stop. So lowering the cell either deadlocked the Tech
  Lead or dropped the D-21 loop bound.
- **The human's decision:** option `park-in-blocked`, chosen by Olger Oeselg on 2026-10-30. At the
  default `block` a named human takes the ticket. At `notify` or `off` the Tech Lead makes no move,
  nothing is re-enqueued, and the ticket stays in `Blocked` (at `notify` with an `observation` per
  Workflow 16 that names its `failed-attempt` note). Workflow 09 raises it for a human after
  `blocked_escalation_days`. A level kept at the re-score stop is a `Move: re-plan`. No step number
  changes, no `## Stop conditions` bullet is added or removed, and no checkpoint tag moves or is
  added.

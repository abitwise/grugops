# Plan 29-11 style dispositions

The thirteen hand-authored checklists put on the writing profile, and four kit files still carrying
vocabulary for a mechanism this project deleted two milestones ago corrected.

With this plan the **checklists part of the governed corpus reaches zero findings over its full
visited denominator** — 354 sentences across thirteen files, and zero `## Steps` bullets, which is a
fact about the corpus rather than an omission and is recorded as one below.

**The judgement is written here; the table below is only its coverage.** A row proves that a changed
clause was considered. It proves nothing about whether the consideration was right — that is a named
human reading these rows against the diff, which is what LANG-03's manual verification is.

## What this plan found, stated before what it did

The checklists part opened at **four findings**, all `guard_sentence_form`
`bare-demonstrative-subject` (WP-06), in four of the thirteen files. `guard_imperative_lexicon`
opened at **zero** — and the zero is not a clean bill, it is an **empty denominator**: no
hand-authored checklist carries a `## Steps` heading, so the imperative predicate derives no element
from this part at all. Recorded here explicitly, because a predicate reporting zero over zero
elements and a predicate reporting zero over a full denominator are different facts and the gate's
own per-part vacuity reasoning is what makes the distinction matter.

**Nine of the thirteen files are byte-unchanged by the style pass.** Every checklist bullet in this
part is an ACCEPTANCE ITEM — a condition that must hold — rather than a procedural step, and WP-04
decides that by section anchor exactly as the guard does. An acceptance item is not reshaped into an
imperative, and none was. The per-section bullet counts are recorded before and after in the SUMMARY
and are equal in every file.

## The generated OWASP checklist — the exclusion confirmed, not inferred

`agent-factory/checklists/security-nfr-checklist.md` is **89,840 bytes, 82 percent of its own
directory, and 345 rows copied verbatim from a third-party standard**. It is excluded from the
governed corpus by the derived `GENERATED` marker (D-42), never by name.

**The confirmation, recorded rather than left to be inferred from the file's absence from the diff.**
Its header region was read — lines 1 to 14 only — and carries the generator's marker on line 4:

```
<!-- GENERATED — do not hand-edit. Re-run: node scripts/generate-asvs-checklist.js
     Source: OWASP ASVS 5.0.0 · OWASP/ASVS @ v5.0.0_release
     Commit: 5cf9b032440be53ce345ab3c130fda46ba1ce7a2 -->
```

The file was **not opened beyond that header and not edited**. A style pass over it would be
reverted on the next `npm run generate:asvs` run, would falsify the document's own claim that "every
row's text is copied verbatim from the standard", and would triple the gate's denominator so the
reported numbers stopped meaning anything.

`GENERATED_EXEMPT` still derives exactly that one path against a two-sided `GENERATED_EXEMPT_COUNT`
of 1, and `GOVERNED_CORPUS_COUNT` is still 47 in four parts.

## The four WP-06 corrections, and why each is the guard's own remedy rather than a way around it

`guard_sentence_form`'s remedy text for this arm reads *"name the antecedent"*, and its own source
states the distinction it decides: *"`This is the drift` is a bare demonstrative subject, while
`This workflow runs at release` is a demonstrative DETERMINER modifying a noun and is correct."*
Each correction below supplies the head noun the bare demonstrative was missing.

| file | before | after | why this shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| `00-index.md` | `These are the gate contracts for grugops.` | `These checklists are the gate contracts for grugops.` | the demonstrative takes its head noun; the two tables below the sentence are the antecedent it names |
| `compliance-checklist.md` | `This is a safety and compliance gate: …` | `The checklist is a safety and compliance gate: …` | a definite noun phrase rather than `This checklist`, because the preceding sentence already opens `Apply this checklist …` and repeating it twice in two sentences is the WP-09 infelicity the alternative would buy |
| `definition-of-done-enterprise.md` | `This is the enterprise superset of the lean Definition of Done.` | `This checklist is the enterprise superset of the lean Definition of Done.` | the file opener has no preceding sentence to repeat, so the demonstrative-determiner form reads directly |
| `playwright-visual-regression-recipe.md` | `This is a reference how-to.` | `This document is a reference how-to.` | `This recipe` and `This file` both repeat a noun from an adjacent sentence; `This document` names the antecedent without the repetition |

**What was available and was refused.** Swapping the bare demonstrative for the pronoun `it` clears
every one of these four findings, because `it` is not a member of the closed demonstrative set the
predicate enumerates. That is clearing a finding by moving the text outside the predicate's token
set while leaving the defect WP-06 exists to catch exactly where it was — the reader still cannot
resolve the subject without the previous sentence. It was not taken in any of the four. This is the
same bar plan 29-08 held when it refused to move a period outside a quotation mark to defeat a
splitter boundary rule.

**No predicate was widened and no scan set was narrowed.** `git diff -- scripts/ package.json` is
empty for this plan.

## The out-of-corpus files, and why they got a vocabulary fix and nothing else

`agent-factory/README.md` and `agent-factory/_commit-convention.md` are **documentation about the
kit rather than instructions an agent executes**, which is the line D-36 draws and the same line
`GOVERNED_CORPUS_EXCLUDED_LOCATIONS` already draws for the packaging documents and the config-dial
documentation. Neither is a member of the 47-file governed corpus.

Style-rewriting either one was available and was **refused**. It would extend the corpus by stealth:
the pinned count is two-sided precisely so the governed set cannot grow silently, and a file that
receives the profile without entering the count is a file the gate does not police and a later
reader believes it does. Both diffs are one noun phrase each, and `GOVERNED_CORPUS_COUNT` is still
47 after this plan.

`agent-factory/README.md` **is** in the LANG-03 watched corpus — it is one of the four public
documents that host safety claims — so its changed clauses carry rows below. The edit sits at line
111, far from both of its registered claim anchors (`C-28-023` at line 14 and `C-28-032` at line
78), and neither anchor's verbatim text is touched.

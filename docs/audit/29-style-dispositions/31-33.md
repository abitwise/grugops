every run and refuses any changed clause that has no row.

The rows below cover the six clauses this plan ADDED **and** the two clauses `31-29` rewrote at the

cell clause by clause, so a two-sentence cell matches neither of its own sentences — those two have

BELOW the recorded number rather than at it. Nothing was narrowed and the base commit was not moved:
both would clear a finding by deleting its evidence.

## Why the workflow changed

`31-VERIFICATION.md` round 7 measured this file's ledger paragraph FALSE at the route it names by
hand:

> States (rewritten this round) that promoteAdmitted and admitAndAppend are the two routes that
> write both a note and a GOV-02 ledger event, and that 'both steps name the same derived
> repository' … — independently measured false of admitAndAppend in this session (the note landed
> in DEST with no ledger line there; the ledger line landed in THIRD with no note there).

**The two sentences the verifier quoted are NOT rewritten, and that is deliberate.** A claim is
corrected by moving the MECHANISM, never by weakening the claim — `D-31`'s own rejected alternative,
restated. Task 1 of this plan moved the mechanism: the owning repository is derived at each route's
ENTRY, above every branch, and `admitAndAppend`'s gated branch appends there. So
`The append precedes the write, and both steps name the same derived repository.` and
`So the destination never holds a human-disposed finding with no ledger line.` are true of the
mechanism now, and they stay as written.

What DID change is what those sentences sit beside, and each change is a claim the mechanism now
holds and the document did not yet state:

**The derivation is no longer a property of one route.** The paragraph said `A re-binding derives
the destination repository…`. Both named routes derive it now, and both derive it at their entry
rather than inside one arm — which is the whole content of `CR-22`. A reader told only about the
re-binding route would still not know why the other route cannot split the two halves.

**The refusal is no longer a property of one arm.** `A destination that does not resolve to a
governed store is refused by name before anything is written.` was true only of the human-stamp-gated
arm: the fall-through returned nineteen lines above the clause. The sentence is kept and a second
one states the property the move installs.

**The boundary is published rather than left silent.** Three new sentences say what the caller's
repository argument answers, where it decides nothing, and — the residual `R-31-33-01` — where an
ordinary admission carrying no human disposition still records itself. The last of those is a
DISCLOSURE, and it is in the workflow because this phase's rule is that a claim and its mechanism are
written down together. A workflow that stated only the closed half would be the claim outrunning the
mechanism inside the edit that exists to stop it.

**Sentence length.** The added sentences are short because the Phase 29 writing profile bounds a
procedural sentence at 20 words and a descriptive one at 25. The prose was split rather than the
corpus narrowed.

**Scope note.** The clauses reported at line 75 are `31-29`'s forgery-price sentences inside the
frozen `## Stop conditions` region. This plan does not touch them and does not re-claim them; they
are dispositioned by `31-29.md`, which wrote them.

## What this file does not settle

- **Whether the default context root belongs to the kit or to the host repository.** That is
  `CR-22`'s fourth position, published as `R-31-33-02`. It is a decision about where the shared
  verified context lives, not a defect, and taking it silently inside a gap-closure plan is the move
  this phase forbids.
- **The two clauses at line 75.** They are `31-29`'s, inside the frozen `## Stop conditions` region,
  and untouched here.
- **Whether the disclosure sentence is read.** `05-pr-quality-gate.md` and every role point at this
  workflow, and nothing in this kit can make an agent obey a pointer. The boundary is legible; being
  read is the host's.

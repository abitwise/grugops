## Why the workflow changed

`31-REVIEW.md` round 8 measured this file's ledger paragraph FALSE at one branch and SILENT at
another, quoting the three sentences that carried the claim:

> The corrected paragraph reads "Each route derives the owning repository from the context store it
> writes the note into. Both halves of the action key on that one answer. The derivation sits at the
> route's entry, above every branch that route takes." Measured on the committed `.js`: false for
> `admitAndAppend`'s gated branch whenever the store is ungoverned (CR-26), and false for its
> non-gated branch always.

**`31-39` moved the mechanism, and the paragraph still did not follow.** Round 7's rule — a claim is
corrected by moving the MECHANISM, never by weakening the claim — is `D-31`'s rejected alternative
restated, and it stands. What round 7 did NOT state is its second half: where a mechanism has more
than one branch, a claim about all of them is falsified by one of them, and the honest form is one
statement per branch. That is what this plan writes.

**The three universals are DELETED rather than softened.** A hedged version of a false universal
reproduces the finding one adverb over. So is the fourth sentence in the list below: `An ordinary
admission carrying no human disposition records itself in the repository whose dial admitted it.` was
`31-33`'s disclosure of `R-31-33-01`, and `31-39` closed the shape it disclosed — the non-gated
branch now records itself in the repository its own STORE derives, not the one whose dial admitted
it. The sentence became false in the round that fixed it, which is `WR-39`'s defect in the other
document.

**Every remaining residual is named INLINE, at the sentence it qualifies.** An agent reading the
workflow learns the boundary at the moment it matters rather than by finding a register. Four ids
appear: `R-31-33-01`, `R-31-33-02`, `R-31-41-01`, `R-31-41-02`.

**The imperative restatement is reconciled, not left silent.** `## Stop conditions` said nothing
about the one decline every path of the re-binding route raises, so a reader following only the stop
conditions would not know to stop for it. That is the same claim-outruns-mechanism defect at a
smaller scale. The section is FROZEN by heading, so each of its five added clauses carries a
`companion` cell naming the section and the reason, per this directory's contract.

**Sentence length.** The added sentences are short because the Phase 29 writing profile bounds a
procedural sentence at 20 words and a descriptive one at 25. The prose was split rather than the
corpus narrowed.

## What this file does not settle

- **Whether the default context root belongs to the kit or to the host repository.** That is
  `CR-22`'s fourth position, published as `R-31-33-02` and named inline in the rewritten paragraph.
  It is a decision about where the shared verified context lives, not a defect, and taking it
  silently inside a gap-closure plan is the move this phase forbids.
- **The three clauses at line 75.** They are `31-29`'s forgery-price sentences, inside the same
  frozen region this plan adds to, and untouched here. They are dispositioned by `29-...`'s own
  round and contribute to the standing 78.
- **Whether the rewritten paragraph is READ.** `05-pr-quality-gate.md` and every role point at this
  workflow, and nothing in this kit can make an agent obey a pointer. The boundary is legible; being
  read is the host's.
- **Whether any disposition above is CORRECT.** This directory records that a change was considered,
  never that the consideration was right. Whether a reworded stop condition still withholds the same
  permission is a human judgement, and the manual verification for LANG-03 is a named human reading
  these rows against the diff they describe.

# Plan 45-10 style dispositions (BR-01, BR-03, BR-05, BR-06, TRC-01; the build-request procedure)

Plan 45-10 writes the build-request procedure once, in
`agent-factory/workflows/20-upstream-sources.md` (Workflow 20). It changes one watched document.
Workflow 20 gains one `## When to use` sentence, two `## Agents involved` bullets (one new, one
widened), two `## Inputs required` bullets, `## Steps` 14 and 15, the new `## Build requests`
section with 13 steps and the escalation paragraph, two widened and two new `## Stop conditions`
bullets, and new text in `## Board moves`, `## Trace updates` and `## Done condition`. Steps 1 to
13 keep their numbers, so no renumbered line needs a kept row. Every form is named by pointing to
`agent-factory/contracts/build-request.md`; none is restated.

The four `## Stop conditions` rows and the `## Done condition` rows carry a `companion` cell naming
the section and the reason. The two widened stop bullets keep their stop; only the program list
grows. The two new stop bullets are untagged and counted in `WORKFLOW_STOP_BULLET_COUNT` 58 in
`scripts/checkpoints.ts`, in the same commit.

The research Pitfall 8 option, a validator warning for a `## Blocked` bullet naming an unknown or
terminal build request, is not adopted (planner decision, plan 45-10). The per-bullet rule fails
toward escalation: a bullet naming an `answered`, `withdrawn` or absent request ages under
`blocked_escalation_days` as usual. The residual risk is accepted as T-45-35.

**The base commit is `e8d7802ccf8ebf428a47703f6001327325e3aea3`**, read from `00-base.md`. It is not
moved by this plan. The gate reported 53 findings in Workflow 20 with these edits and no rows, 4 of
them frozen, and 0 once these rows were written. The rows were generated from the gate's own clause
segmentation (`changedClauses`, `readDispositionRows`, `rowMatches` and the `segmentClauses` split),
so each `after` cell is the clause the gate derives. Where a clause rewords an earlier one, the
`before` cell gives the earlier text.

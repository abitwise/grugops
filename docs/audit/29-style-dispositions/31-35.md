## Why the recipe changed

`CR-25` of `31-REVIEW.md`, independently reproduced and bisected twice — by the review's own code
review and by `31-VERIFICATION.md` round 7 — and re-reproduced in this plan's own scratch before any
source byte moved. `frameworkSurface` bounded its breadth-first walk at `SURFACE_NODE_BOUND = 4096`
and `SURFACE_DEPTH_BOUND = 6` and returned a PARTIAL surface with no signal, so every framework
declaration past the bound resolved `foreign` — accept. Neither bound appeared in the recipe's
boundary list or in `UNRESOLVABLE_CALLEE_RESIDUALS`: the recipe published a ban strength the
mechanism did not deliver, and published no limit that would have told a reader why.

Measured at the runnable's own entry against the committed `.js`, before the change and after it,
each with its own `tsc --noEmit` exit code (per-file layout, local-alias head):

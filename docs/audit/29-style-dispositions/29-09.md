The middle seven workflows — `06-uat-pack.md`, `07-backlog-refinement.md`, `08-sprint-planning.md`,
`09-daily-sweep.md`, `10-sprint-review.md`, `11-retro.md` and `12-release.md` — put on the writing
profile: every `## Steps` bullet on the canonical step form (WP-01), every procedural sentence at or
under 20 words and every descriptive sentence at or under 25 (WP-02, WP-03, WP-04), and the banned
constructions removed (WP-05, WP-06).

**The judgement is written here; the table below is only its coverage.** A row proves that a changed
clause was considered. It proves nothing about whether the consideration was right — that is a named
human reading these rows against the diff, which is what LANG-03's manual verification is.

This plan follows plan 29-08's conversion patterns rather than inventing new ones. The four
transformations that account for the rows are the ones `29-08.md` argues in full; what follows names
only what is new or decided differently here.

## The one mechanic this batch uses that 29-08 did not

**A split sentence does not have to become a split bullet.** `deriveElements()` pushes ONE bullet per
list line and then splits that line into sentences, so a `## Steps` bullet carrying three conforming
sentences is one bullet and three sentences. Every over-long step in this batch was therefore fixed
**inside its own bullet**, and the `## Steps` bullet denominator is **unchanged in all seven files**
(4 / 7 / 13 / 7 / 5 / 4 / 6, before and after).

That is a deliberate difference from 29-08, where five of six files' bullet denominators rose. Both
routes reach the same conforming text; this one leaves the denominator that T-29-51 is about
provably fixed, which makes the finding movement in this batch read against a constant.

refused.

**Refused: turning the field list into a table or a fence.** Either would have moved nine bullets out

## The recording convention this register inherits

**Why a row exists for text that did not change.** A workflow paragraph is a **single source line**.
`git diff --unified=0` reports that whole line as touched when any one clause on it moves, and the
gate then derives every clause on it. So an unchanged sentence sitting on the same line as a changed
one gets a row, marked as unchanged. That is 29-07's and 29-08's accounting, unchanged here.

**The pipe character.** `readDispositionRows()` splits a row on `|`, so a cell containing a literal
pipe fails the seven-column check and the row is **never read**. One clause in `12-release.md` quotes
`` `READY_TO_RELEASE | BLOCKED | RELEASED` ``; its rows write `/` in place of `|`.
`normalizeSentence()` folds every non-alphanumeric character to a space, so the two normalize
identically and the clause match is unaffected.

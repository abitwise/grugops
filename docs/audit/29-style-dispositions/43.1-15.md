# Phase 43.1 plan 15 style dispositions (the workflows name the repository and the deploy path, WR-08 and IN-10)

Plan 43.1-15 closes the workflow part of review findings WR-08 and IN-10, gap G-43.1-8. Plan 43.1-11 made Workflows 05 and
12 say the production environment holds a deploy only when the deploy credentials live solely in it: a stored deploy secret
lives only in its secrets, and an OIDC trust policy accepts only tokens whose subject names it. That condition had two gaps.

2026-11-20, in UAT test 8 (`.planning/phases/43.1-close-v3-0-p1-debt/43.1-UAT.md`), to fix both in this phase. Plans
43.1-13 and 43.1-14 carried the wider condition to `install/README.md` § 5, the floor model, `docs/GUARANTEES.md`, the pages
and the kit docs. This plan carries it to Workflow 05 step 3 and Workflow 12's `## When to use` paragraph.

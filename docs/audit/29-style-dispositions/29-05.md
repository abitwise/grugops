# Plan 29-05 style dispositions

The first seven role files rewritten onto the canonical role skeleton: `## One job` owns the what as
one sentence, `## Caveman prompt` owns identity and attitude, `## Responsibilities` owns the how, and
`## Hard limits` is the only place a prohibition appears (D-19). The caveman block is the section that
gives up its content (D-09), the second-person copula opener is rewritten rather than exempted (D-41),
and every role gains one when-absent config fallback sentence in `## Reads` (D-30) — added only after
that role's removals landed and were measured (D-37).

Every file in this plan is on the `docs/audit/28-safety-surface-exclusions.md` list. Each row below is
therefore a deliberate sentence-level judgement, recorded here rather than a file-level assumption.

Three clause groups repeat across the rows and are worth reading once rather than seven times.

- **The `## Reads` config bullet clauses.** Appending the D-30 sentence changes the whole bullet LINE,
  so the gate re-derives every clause on that line. The clauses named `Unchanged text` below are
  byte-identical before and after; they carry a row because the line moved, not because the words did.
- **The fallback sentence itself** is intentionally identical in all seven files. The uniqueness guard
  is intra-file (D-21), so that repetition is correct single-sourcing rather than duplication.
- **A deleted caveman line or `## Responsibilities` item whose clause is FROZEN.** `## Hard limits` is
  frozen positionally, and a restatement of one of its clauses elsewhere in the file normalizes to the
  same frozen clause. Deleting the restatement is the D-19 remedy, and the companion cell records that
  `## Hard limits` keeps the clause unchanged and becomes its sole home.

# Plan 29-06 style dispositions

The second batch of eight role files rewritten onto the canonical role skeleton, following the same
rules plan 29-05 proved: `## One job` owns the what as one sentence, `## Caveman prompt` owns
identity and attitude, `## Responsibilities` owns the how, and `## Hard limits` is the only place a
prohibition appears (D-19). The caveman block is the section that gives up its content (D-09), the
second-person copula opener is rewritten rather than exempted (D-41), and every role gains one
when-absent config fallback sentence in `## Reads` (D-30) — added only after that role's removals
landed and were measured (D-37).

`greenfield-mapper.md` and `orchestrator.md` are on the
`docs/audit/28-safety-surface-exclusions.md` list, as is every other role file in this batch. Each
row below is therefore a deliberate sentence-level judgement, recorded here rather than a file-level
assumption.

The `## Responsibilities` deletion rule is the one 29-05 wrote down, applied unchanged: **an item
goes when its SUBJECT is a boundary `## Hard limits` already states, and stays when its subject is
an act that `## Hard limits` merely bounds.** Where an item's subject is an act but it carries a
prohibition *clause*, the clause is dropped and the item stays.

Three clause groups repeat across the rows and are worth reading once rather than eight times.

- **The `## Reads` config bullet clauses.** Appending the D-30 sentence changes the whole bullet
  LINE, so the gate re-derives every clause on that line. The clauses named `Unchanged text` below
  are byte-identical before and after; they carry a row because the line moved, not because the
  words did.
- **The fallback sentence itself** is intentionally identical in all fifteen rewritten files. The
  uniqueness guard is intra-file (D-21), so that repetition is correct single-sourcing rather than
  duplication.
- **A deleted caveman line or `## Responsibilities` item whose clause is FROZEN.** `## Hard limits`
  is frozen positionally, and a restatement of one of its clauses elsewhere in the file normalizes
  to the same frozen clause. Deleting the restatement is the D-19 remedy, and the companion cell
  records that `## Hard limits` keeps the clause unchanged and becomes its sole home.

**One frozen intersection in this plan**, named by the gate: `system-analyst.md:16`
`do not choose framework`. It carries its companion cell below. In this batch, as in 29-05, **no
line inside any `## Hard limits` section was edited** — what moved is a duplicate somewhere else in
the file.

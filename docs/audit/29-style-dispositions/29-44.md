# Plan 29-44 style dispositions

One clause, in one role document, rephrased because a **prohibition got wider** — not because a
style pass reached it. `guard_banned_claims` stopped conditioning three of its literals on a
hand-authored verb list (round 6, D-48 extended by D-53), and the bare term `understand` became a
finding on the one line of correct kit prose that carries it.

**The judgement is written here; the table below is only its coverage.** A row proves that a changed
clause was considered. It proves nothing about whether the consideration was right — that is a named
human reading these rows against the diff, which is what LANG-03's manual verification is.

## What forced the change, stated before what was done

Round 5 replaced a six-phrase comprehension enumeration with a rule conditional on a seven-stem
hand-authored benefit-verb list. That relocated the enumeration from the phrase slot to the verb
slot; it did not delete it, and five plants defeated it at exit 0 with the planted file never named.
Round 6 deleted the conditional half on **both** affected groups rather than moving it a third time.

The admission cost was measured **before** the deletion, through the gate's own
`countBannedClaimOccurrences` over the 115-document corpus plan 29-43 left, and independently
re-taken with `grep -a -i` over the same derived path list — the two agreeing on all three terms:

| bare term | occurrences | inside the one named exemption region | new findings |
|---|---|---|---|
| `comprehension` | 2 | 2 | 0 |
| `Simplified Technical English` | 2 | 2 | 0 |
| `understand` | 1 | 0 | **1** |

**One line in the whole kit pays for the change**, and it is the line dispositioned below. The RED
was captured from the live tree before a word was rephrased, so the sequence is evidence rather than
a fixture:

```
agent-factory/roles/incident-responder.md:29:103 — banned comprehension literal "understand" —
"1. Stop the bleeding first — apply or recommend the immediate mitigation that limits harm, before
you understand the cause. Diagnosis can wait; the bleeding cannot."
```

## The fix that was available and was refused

Three weakenings would each have made the original sentence pass without touching a byte of it:

1. **skip fenced blocks** — the line is not fenced, but a matcher that skipped them would delete the
   check for every document that quotes an example;
2. **match whole words only** — `understand` is a whole word on that line, so this would not even
   have cleared it; it would only have narrowed the term's reach to the plural, the gerund and the
   negated form, deleting three quarters of the prohibition to reach one sentence;
3. **skip lines below some marker** — a positional hatch with no named region behind it, which is
   the second exemption this gate refuses by name.

**None was taken.** All three are named and forbidden in the gate's own forbidden-alternative
paragraph, and that paragraph is byte-unchanged across this plan. The fourth non-fix — admitting a
second exemption region for this file — is refused by the same paragraph: if a second region ever
earns an exemption the answer is a second named triple with its own reason, never a weaker
predicate, and an operational instruction in a `## Responsibilities` list has not earned one.

## Why the rephrase supplies the meaning rather than evading the matcher

The clause states a temporal precondition: mitigate **before** the cause is established. The
original stated it as a **mental state** of the reader (`before you understand the cause`); the
replacement states it as the **act** the role actually defers (`before the cause has been
diagnosed`) — which is the same act the very next sentence names when it says *Diagnosis can wait*.
The two halves of the step now use one word for one thing, which is the profile's own rule, and the
instruction is neither narrowed nor made conditional: mitigation still comes first, under
uncertainty, in every case.

This is not the swap this phase refused seventeen times across plans 29-08 through 29-12. Those
refusals were of a move that leaves the **defect** where it is and steps the words outside a
predicate's reach. There is no defect here to leave behind — the sentence makes no claim of any
kind, and the gate's over-reach on the bare term is an accepted, measured and named cost, carried
with the standing remedy *rephrase the sentence, or admit a narrower term with its own measurement,
never weaken the matcher*. The rephrase is that remedy, applied.

## What the change was

`CR-16` of `31-REVIEW.md`: `promoteAdmitted`'s origin trust was a two-component basename comparison
that any single `mkdir -p` satisfied. `D-25` replaced it with ONE canonical origin form — a
recognised SHAPE **conjoined with** ROOT ANCHORING — because a shape alone accepts a directory the
caller just created, and anchoring alone accepts any directory under a real root. The sentences at
line 56 are that form stated for a reader; the sentences at line 75 are the stop condition that says
what the form does NOT refuse, and at what price.

**Line 75 is a FROZEN structural section** (`## Stop conditions`, `structuralSections`), so every row
for it carries a filled `companion` cell naming the section and the reason. There is no override tier
and no placeholder spelling that satisfies it.

## Why the recipe changed

`CR-23` of `31-REVIEW.md`, reproduced independently by `31-VERIFICATION.md` round 7 and again in
this plan's own scratch before any source byte moved. The recipe published `describe` as a banned
head segment, quoted by value from `BANNED_MODIFIER_HEADS`, over a mechanism that could not reach
it: the `D-30 (2)` identity cutover made the `foreign` answer terminal, and `@playwright/test`
declares no top-level `describe`, so the head was dead for exactly the case its own comment retains
it for. The same held for `expect.soft` whenever `expect` came from another assertion library.

Measured at the runnable's own entry against the committed `.js`, each with its own `tsc --noEmit`
exit code, before the change and after it:

```

undeclared head                     1 finding(s) EXIT=1 -> 1 finding(s) EXIT=1   tsc 2
framework head                      1 finding(s) EXIT=1 -> 1 finding(s) EXIT=1   tsc 0
```

A named human answered `split-foreign-by-provenance` on 2026-09-11. `D-35` in `31-CONTEXT.md`
carries the answer verbatim, the two refused options, and the record that the DISCRIMINANT's form
is the executor's choice on the table above rather than the human's words.

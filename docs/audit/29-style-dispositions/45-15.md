Plan 45-15 gives a floor conflict found at bootstrap a path that can run (gap G-45-9b, review
finding WR-06). Workflow 20 stays the single statement of the floor rule; Workflow 00 names no floor
and points at it. No role file changes.

`agent-factory/workflows/00-bootstrap-greenfield.md` (Workflow 00), `## Steps` step 1: the plan
45-14 sentence with the semicolon becomes three sentences. The Greenfield Mapper applies no
decision that conflicts with a floor, leaves that stack item `UNKNOWN - verify` and not filled from
`default_stack`, and reports the decision and its floor to the Orchestrator, which routes the
conflict per Workflow 20. The other sentences of step 1 are unchanged.

request at step 6 per Workflow 20, then the bootstrap stops for a named human before step 7. It is
untagged because no dial value may lower a floor (ARCH-02). `WORKFLOW_STOP_BULLET_COUNT` moves
58 -> 59 in `scripts/checkpoints.ts` in the same commit.

`agent-factory/workflows/20-upstream-sources.md` (Workflow 20), `## Upstream architecture`: the
first floor-path bullet routes a conflict the Greenfield Mapper reports to the Architect, the same
route the Orchestrator's own findings take. A new seventh bullet says what changes at bootstrap:
BA/PM files at Workflow 00 step 6, naming in `## Blocks` each ticket that needs the item, and writes
that ticket first when none does; Workflow 00 waits before step 7 until a named human lifts the
stop. The closing line "The floor does not move." is unchanged.

Task 2, Workflow 20 `## Upstream architecture`, the bootstrap provenance bullet: its second sentence
no longer limits the Greenfield Mapper to recording provenance. The Mapper records which source and
pin gave each stack item, and reports a floor conflict as the floor path says (WR-06). After this edit
the gate reported 1 more finding and 0 once its row was written.

moved by this plan. Workflow 20 and Workflow 00 step 1 are new since the base, so each changed clause

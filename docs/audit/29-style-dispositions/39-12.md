# Plan 39-12 style dispositions (gap closure round 1; CR-02 and the folded warnings)

Plan 39-12 is the second plan of the first Phase 39 gap closure round. It closes CR-02 (TIER-04)
and folds in WR-01, WR-02, WR-04, WR-05 and WR-07. It edits these watched documents:
`agent-factory/workflows/04-ticket-to-pr.md`, `agent-factory/roles/tech-lead.md`,
`agent-factory/roles/software-engineer.md` and `agent-factory/roles/orchestrator.md`.

- **CR-02** (TIER-04): after a gate `BLOCKED_NEEDS_FIX` with the self-fix budget spent, Workflow 04's
  numbered steps sent the ticket to the Tech Lead and on to a re-enqueue with no human in between.
  The only human sat in a `## Stop conditions` bullet that cited Workflow 05 for a Tech Lead step
  Workflow 05 does not name. Step 15 now stops for a named human before the ticket returns to
  `Ready for Dev`, step 16 waits for that human, and the stop bullet says only what Workflow 05
  says. Workflow 05 itself is not edited, and no checkpoint tag moves or is added.
- **WR-01** (TIER-04, D-19, D-21): the Tech Lead's moves on a blocked ticket are bounded. Each ticket
  gets at most one escalation and, besides it, at most one other move (a re-plan, a split or an
  Architect pass). A re-score that raises the level is the escalation. The Tech Lead counts earlier
  moves from the ticket's `decision` notes, and once both are spent the next block stops for a
  named human under the existing `escalate_unadjudicable_result` tag.
- **WR-07** (TIER-01, D-05, D-07): the Tech Lead writes the dev tasks before it scores them, so the
  rubric's F1 and F2 read a written dev plan. `tech-lead.md` swaps its Responsibilities items 2 and
  3, and Workflow 04 step 2 states the order. The rubric is not in the watched corpus.

`## Stop conditions` is FROZEN by heading. Every row in it carries a `companion` cell that names the
section and the reason.

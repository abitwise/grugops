# Plan 39-19 style dispositions (gap closure round 4; G-39-71, W1-W4)

Plan 39-19 is the first plan of the fourth Phase 39 gap closure round, the fourth and last round
under the four-round cap. It closes UAT gap G-39-71, which carries verification finding W1 (and,
through the same rewrite, W2, W3 and W4) from `39-VERIFICATION.md`. It edits three watched
documents: `agent-factory/workflows/04-ticket-to-pr.md` (`## Agents involved`, steps 15-17, `## Board
moves` and the used-up bullet in `## Stop conditions`), `agent-factory/roles/tech-lead.md` (`## Reads`,
`## Responsibilities` and `## Hard limits`), and the `## Responsibilities` of
`agent-factory/roles/ba-pm.md` and `agent-factory/roles/architect-design.md`. It also edits
`agent-factory/config/factory.config.md`, which the gate does not watch.

- **W1** (TIER-04, D-19..D-21): Workflow 04 step 15, the tagged used-up stop, tech-lead.md and the
  factory.config.md escalate paragraph disagreed at `notify` and `off`. W2 asked how a parked ticket
  leaves `Blocked`, W3 asked for one human stop with a resume record, and W4 asked that the human see
  the move before confirming it.
- **The human's decision (D-21 amended):** Olger Oeselg, 2026-10-31, Phase 39 UAT test 71: "W1
  decision, when Tech lead can't solve it then decision should be made to send it back to ba/pm or
  architect (or suitable role who can solve the reasons why the ticket is blocked). If there is
  disagreement between what is the solution only then turn to human." / "And before bringing to
  human, try to solve the disagreement 3 times. Similar approach should be elsewhere as well. Human's
  role is stakeholder, if fundamental changes are not required to requirements and rules then human
  attention is not needed." / disagreement = "When roles have argued 3 times and not found
  resolution." Once the Tech Lead's options are used up, it hands the ticket to the role whose job
  covers its cause, at every value of `checkpoints.escalate_unadjudicable_result`, at most three times
  for one cause. A named human is asked only after three disputed rounds on one cause, or when the
  fix changes a requirement or a rule fundamentally. At `notify` or `off` that final stop parks the
  ticket in `Blocked` with no move. No `## Stop conditions` bullet is added or removed, and no
  checkpoint tag moves or is added.

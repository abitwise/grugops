<objective>
Make the platform-shapes control observe its own effect, and make every printed outcome label name the
outcome that actually happened.

Two findings, one shape — a record that reports a conclusion it did not measure:

- **WR-41** — at the note position the control's verdict is set on any non-throwing call, and the
  ordinary staging plants bytes identical to what the writer would write, so the drive passes through
  the chokepoint's identical-bytes no-op branch. Nothing is written, the disk is never inspected, and
  the row prints its ordinary-outcome label.
- **IN-20** — every outcome that is not the expected one prints the refusal label, so a crashed
  driver and a genuine refusal are indistinguishable in the table two rounds have compared against.

Purpose: this module's rows are evidence a future verification round reads. A row that reports a
label it did not measure is the same defect as a workflow sentence stronger than its mechanism, in the
artifact a round uses to decide whether it is looking at progress.
Output: a note-position control that reads its own target, a derived outcome-label vocabulary with an
asserted cardinality, both watched failing against confirmed mirrors, and decision `D-43` recording
the changed label semantics for the benefit of a round comparing transcripts across them.
</objective>

<objective>
Close gap 2 of `31-VERIFICATION.md` round 3 (CR-08 of `31-REVIEW.md`) — a regression `31-09`
introduced while correctly closing round 2's CR-05, independently reproduced by the round-3 verifier
against the committed `.js`.

Making `appendNote` consult `admit()` unconditionally is right, and D-01's evidence floor depends on
it. But `compactor.promote` is a thin pass-through to `appendNote`, and Workflow 18 names it as the
only prescribed route for carrying a note forward through compaction. So a note a human already
legitimately disposed at the ORIGIN — written through `admitAndAppend`'s gated, pre-admitted branch,
disposed by the un-forgeable admission-guard hook — is now REFUSED, unchanged, at the DESTINATION by
`admit()`'s frozen human-stamp arm, for the same structural reason that branch skips the authority in
the first place: this tier cannot verify a self-authored `human:NAME` stamp. Workflow 18 step 6's only
recovery, "degrade to a claim carrying `confidence: UNKNOWN - verify`", would therefore silently
discard a human's adjudicated high-severity security, architecture or release disposition on every
compaction while the dial is active — in exactly the regulated-team posture this project's governance
dial exists to serve.

`31-09`'s own blast-radius table enumerated this call site and dispositioned it "admits, unchanged
shape ... for every kind" without driving the case that changed; the round's only promotion probe used
a fabricated `§14-gate` stamp, not a legitimate human one. The doctrine correction is the one this
repository's history already records: **ask how a gate is REACHED, not only what it refuses.** A
re-write reaches a predicate built only to decide new admissions.

Purpose: promotion of an already-admitted note is not a new admission, it is a RE-BINDING. This plan
gives that re-binding its own route, gated by a PROOF over bytes that already exist at the origin
rather than by a parameter an agent can set — and derives both the caller axis and the
proof's-own-decline axis so the next shape cannot land silently.

Output: an exported `promoteAdmitted` in `scripts/context-io.ts` with a byte-equality proof and a
fail-closed dial read, a thin pass-through in `scripts/compactor.ts`, the extended derived caller-set
and writer-family matrix assertions with their watched-fail mirrors, the human-disposed promotion test
the verifier asked for, a rewritten Workflow 18 steps 4-6, and D-19 recorded as a dated gap-closure
decision.

**Ordering note:** wave 2 and `depends_on: ["31-13"]` for one reason only — both plans append a dated
decision to `.planning/phases/31-autonomous-manual-testing/31-CONTEXT.md`. The two gaps are logically
independent and the code files are disjoint.
</objective>

<assumption_delta_decision>
The deterministic assumption-delta detector fired on this phase (`kind: optional`). Re-read against
THIS round's scope, the real delta is not the optional attended lane — that is pre-existing Phase-31
design, untouched here — it is that **admission acquires a second case**: an admission decision about a
note used to have one shape ("is this new note admissible?") and now has two ("is this a new admission,
or a faithful re-binding of one that already happened?").

- **The noun that is now primary:** *an admission decision about a note*, whose two variants are a NEW
  ADMISSION and a RE-BINDING OF AN ADMITTED ORIGIN NOTE.
- **Decision: `add-alongside`,** accepted as debt. `promoteAdmitted` sits beside `appendNote` rather
  than the re-binding case being promoted INTO `admit()` as a new arm. Rationale: `admit()`'s
  human-stamp arm is FROZEN by an earlier phase's forged-stamp backstop, and the round-3 verifier's
  own `missing:` prescribes the separate proof-gated route rather than a new arm. Promoting the general
  representation would mean editing a frozen safety arm to accept an origin proof, which is a strictly
  larger blast radius than the gap requires and would put a fifth round at risk.
- **What would force a later promote:** a THIRD re-write route appearing (a second consumer needing the
  same proof), or any need for the audit ledger to record a re-binding as a first-class admission event
  rather than as a pre-admitted skip. Either makes "one authority decides both variants" cheaper than
  keeping two routes in step.
- **Companion invariant test (accepted, and it is Task 1's own acceptance criterion):** a note admitted
  at an origin and promoted unchanged round-trips to the destination under every value of the
  `human_admission` dial. That test goes red the instant a future change reintroduces the
  single-variant assumption.
</assumption_delta_decision>

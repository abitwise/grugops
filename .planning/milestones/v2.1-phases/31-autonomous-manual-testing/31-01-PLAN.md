against, an `artifact-ref` evidence note carries `sha` + `gate_run` + `content_hash` (D-01, D-02),

field at all, so D-03's comparison has no left operand. Every other UATX-04 task is downstream of
this. The reserved-identity economy that makes UATX-01 true (only the gate carve-out authors a
`§14-gate` note) already exists and must still hold after the signature change.

Output: a `scripts/context-io.ts` (+ its committed `.js`) whose verdict records a SHA, whose
composer emits three artifact-ref-only provenance scalars, and whose admission authority refuses a
stale-SHA evidence note; the contract doc updated in the same breath as the composer.

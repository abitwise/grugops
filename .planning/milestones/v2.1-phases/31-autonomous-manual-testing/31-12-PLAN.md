<objective>
Close the third `missing:` item of gap 2 in `31-VERIFICATION.md` round 2: add the reverse
cross-check WR-13 names, so that every `skip`/`only`/`fixme`/`fail`-shaped member reachable on the
declared `@playwright/test` surface is either refused by the ban decision or named as a residual,
asserted by partition cardinality rather than by one-directional membership.

Purpose: the harness 31-06 shipped is presented as the cross-check that would have caught the gap,
and it asks exactly one question — for each member of the ban set, does the declared surface export
its head and does the call type-check. That answers "is every banned spelling real?" It cannot answer
"is every real modifier banned?", which is the direction CR-01 was and CR-06 still was. The reverse
check is additionally bounded by the stub, which declares no routing groups and no inverting
modifier, so even a reversed harness derived from the shipped stub would report complete today. This
plan supplies both halves: a surface that carries what CR-06 names, and a partition over it whose
cardinality is asserted.

Output: a derived surface enumeration read from the declared type by the TypeScript checker; a total
partition of it into refused-by-rule and dispositioned-with-a-reason; a premise block that catches a
short walk as well as an empty one; two watched failures proving the partition discriminates and that
the rule generalises to a routing group nobody enumerated; and a recipe and stub header that state
the claim at exactly the strength it has.
</objective>

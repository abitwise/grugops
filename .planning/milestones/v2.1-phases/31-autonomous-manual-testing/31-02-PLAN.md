<objective>
Build the target-side UAT spec-integrity runnable: a materialized `tools/grugops/uat-spec-integrity.js`
that derives the `*.uat.spec.ts` set (D-05), parses each one with the TARGET repo's own `typescript`
(D-13), refuses D-14's three arms of conditional and caught assertions over the AST, and loud-skips
with a distinct exported marker and exit 2 whenever the parser or the browser lane is unusable
(D-15). Requirements UATX-05 and UATX-06.

Purpose: UATX-06's whole point is that the claim matches the mechanism — a regex would false-positive
on the strings, comments and selectors a Playwright spec is full of, and would let the recipe claim
more than the checker decides. UATX-05's whole point is that an absent browser is loud and leaves the
UAT `pending`, never a silent pass.

Output: a `node:`-builtins-only runnable authored in TypeScript, compiled to committed `.js`,
delivered to host repos through the existing `RUNNABLES` materialization path, with a fixture corpus
that proves discrimination in both directions.
</objective>

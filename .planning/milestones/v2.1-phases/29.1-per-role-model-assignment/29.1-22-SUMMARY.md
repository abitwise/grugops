## Decisions Made

1. **Change the unit, not the character class.** Rounds 3 and 4 both answered "is this capture admissible" and neither asked "which occurrences is the predicate even asked about". Widening the class would have been a third answer to the second question.
2. **Delete the second grammar.** `CONFIG_NAMED_PATH` is gone, not kept beside `configReferencesIn`. A module carrying two answers to one question is how all three bypasses were reachable.
3. **Write a floor that cannot fire.** The premise floor is unreachable by construction today. It is the assertion that would have named R3-CR-01 the day the two units first parted.
4. **Derive the set of set-entry points.** Fixing the two the verifier named would have been the set-literal-drift class.
5. **Do not fix, and do not rewrite, the pre-existing frontmatter false refusal.** See Deviation 1.

R2-WR-04: `agent-factory/packaging/subagent.frontmatter.md`'s `model` bullet says the resolver reads
the block from the first of two configuration locations that exists, and only when the first is
absent entirely from a bundled default inside the kit — and then, one clause later, says the bullet
names the shape and deliberately does not restate the rule. That first clause IS the rule:
`factory.config.md`'s "the first of those two files that EXISTS wins WHOLE" in weaker words. The
document states a rule and asserts that it did not. The oracle cannot see it, because
`PRECEDENCE_RULE_ANCHOR` pins one verbatim literal and any paraphrase clears it — while the module's
header discloses a normalization residual on that matcher and does not disclose the far larger one,
that "exactly one document states the rule" is enforced against one spelling of the rule rather than
against the rule.

The review offered an either/or. This plan takes both halves, because each alone leaves a defect
standing: stop restating, AND stop claiming not to restate. The second half is not fussiness — a
document asserting a totality about its own prose is exactly the shape D-59 ruled undecidable in this
repository, and D-16 already applies that ruling to MODEL-07 one requirement over. A claim no
mechanism can decide is held as content with its residual disclosed, or it is not made.

R2-IN-03: `scripts/adapters-freshness.test.ts:531` asserts a multi-paragraph refusal contains the
digit two, which almost any output satisfies — including a temp path or a line number — leaving the
case's discrimination carried entirely by a case-insensitive word match. The sibling at 552-554 does
the same with two counts.

Output: an authority that owes a reader the shape and points at the rule, an oracle whose claim
matches its mechanism with the residual disclosed and its direction stated, a derived assertion that
catches the paraphrase class, and two freshness cases that assert sentences rather than digits.
</objective>

Make the prose agree with the mechanism, in one place, with the other places pointing at it.

MODEL-06 asks that the Claude-Code-only scope be STATED rather than implied: per-subagent
model selection exists on Claude Code alone, the other four host CLIs have no such concept, and
`CLAUDE.md`'s "What NOT to Use" row against non-default wrappers is amended to name this
mechanism as the documented reason rather than being left to contradict it. D-13 gives that

source that already documents the adapter's model choice — and makes the config documentation
point at it rather than restate it. Three surfaces each carrying their own statement is the
failure this shape exists to prevent: they can all be true individually and disagree with each
other.

left to disagree. Four sites in that file carry the old unconditional claim, two of them inside
fenced frontmatter specimens that must keep matching what the generator emits under the shipped
zero-config default.

voice: until the follow-on delivery phase lands, a per-repo `models` block is inert for an
installed target. The mechanism resolves and emits correctly in-kit; the adapters an installed
repo loads are whatever the kit shipped. That is a disclosed limitation of the shipped
increment, and stating it is part of this phase rather than something a user discovers.

Output: one authority, two pointers, one disclosed limitation, and a cross-surface oracle that
asserts the pointer rather than the prose.

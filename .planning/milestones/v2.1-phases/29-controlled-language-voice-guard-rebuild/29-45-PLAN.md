<objective>
Close the findings that are all one shape: **a property asserted in the wrong place** — and dispose the
one whose subject this round deleted out from under it.

`WR-06` — **moot, and that is the finding.** The case is named "the gate REFUSES a conditional member
declared with an empty marker array," and nothing in the gate refuses it; the first authoring of this plan
moved the refusal into the gate, which was the right fix for the tree as it then stood. D-53 then removed
`requiresOnSameLine` from `BannedClaimLiteral` entirely, so an empty-marker member is no longer a shape the
type admits. Shipping the guard clause now would put a loop in `runAll()` that can never fire and count it
in a PASS line — which is AP-1, the exact defect WR-06 was raised about, re-created by its own remedy. So
the refusal does **not** ship. What ships instead is an explicit disposition (**discharged by deleting the
mechanism**) plus a type-level tripwire, because a finding whose remedy silently evaporates is
indistinguishable from a finding that was dropped, and this round exists to correct precisely that.

`WR-01` — the suppressed count's sub-breakdown reads six plus three plus two against a pin of twelve. The
round-5 measurement was six, two and four. The twelve was derived from a run and was right; the
sub-breakdown was typed, inside the paragraph that opens by saying the entrants were derived through the
gate's own counter rather than by reading the prose. **And both figures are now stale**: plan 29-43 widened
the corpus and plan 29-44 made three terms unconditional, so the standard-name column gained the two
disclaimer lines and the total is predicted to be fourteen. That is exactly why the remedy is not a
corrected sentence — a corrected sentence rotted twice in three rounds. It is to make the breakdown an
**output of the traversal that already computes the total**, derived after both predecessors have landed.

`IN-01` — a magic negative in a field whose contract is "the measurement", meaning "not measured", in the
module that refuses that conflation everywhere else. `IN-03` — an equality that is genuinely non-circular
and no longer pins a number, with the loss unstated.

And `WR-04` — a control-byte assertion over two files, standing beside a repo-wide gate that already
decides the NUL half over every tracked path. The mechanism existed and covered 1588 files. What failed
was that nobody ran it before believing two greps. "We added an assertion" is a weaker and less
transferable conclusion than "run the repo-wide gate before believing a grep", and a weaker duplicate of
an existing authority is the D-24 shape this phase closed one module over.

**This plan still earns its own wave.** WR-06's remedy evaporating removes work but not the plan: WR-01's
breakdown must be derived AFTER 29-43's corpus widening and 29-44's deletions have both landed, which is
this wave and no earlier; IN-01, IN-03 and WR-04 are untouched by D-53 and each stands on its own; and the
test surface 29-44 hands over — the retired cardinality apparatus and the cases its deletion made vacuous —
needs an owner. Folding it into a neighbour would put the breakdown derivation in the same commit as the
deletions it must measure after.

Purpose: after plan 29-44 the prohibition is right. This plan makes the gate publish its own numbers and
lets the type hold what a runtime refusal no longer can, so the next round has less prose to trust.

Output: WR-06 disposed with a type-level tripwire in place of a dead guard clause; the breakdown published
by the run; the sentinel expressed in the type; the growth made visible; and one authority for the
control-byte class, with the boundary declared.
</objective>

<objective>
Narrow the sentence `guard_banned_claims` publishes about itself, so the gate stops making a totality
claim its mechanism does not support.

The gate's PASS-line header at `scripts/check-banned-claims.ts:2103` opens with a quantifier over the
shipped kit and over the public documents. The mechanism underneath it decides something much smaller:
whether any member of a 22-entry pinned literal list appears on any single physical line of a derived
document set, outside registry-anchored blocks inside one named region. Seven verification rounds have
each closed one axis between those two statements and exposed the next — an unlisted benefit verb, then
an unlisted conformance verb, then a hard-wrapped multi-word literal — because the distance between them
is not one defect, it is an unbounded class.

**A prohibition that publishes a wider scope than its mechanism is the exact defect LANG-04 exists to
prevent, currently committed by LANG-04's own guard.** Fixing that is closing LANG-04, not conceding it
(D-55).

The second PASS line already publishes the honest content — the derived corpus and its six parts, the
pinned literals matched unconditionally, the one content-bound exemption region with both its pins, and
the registry-anchored blocks. This plan makes the FIRST line agree with it.

Purpose: after this, a reader who sees `ALL CHECKS PASSED` from this gate knows exactly which question
was answered, and the residual axes stop being things a green run quietly implies away.

Output: one narrowed header, one narrowed docblock, two permanent cases that hold the narrowing in both
directions, and a derived table of every place in the tree that states this prohibition's scope.
</objective>

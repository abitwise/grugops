<objective>
Build the two shared authorities this phase's other twelve plans stand on, wire both role-prose
guards through them, and prove the machine end-to-end by watching it fail RED against the real tree
with a measured transcript.

This is the tracer. It touches every code layer the phase modifies: the model module
(`scripts/voice-model.ts`), the shared AP-1 mechanism (`scripts/vacuity.ts`), the guard aggregator
(`scripts/check-foundation-guards.ts`), the hermetic fixture harness, and the committed `.js`
freshness contract. Nothing downstream of it can be built correctly until the one fence reader
exists, because the whole point of LANG-07 is that there is exactly one.

Three decisions define the shape. D-22 makes `voice-model.ts` the single authority and D-24 makes it
*compose* `frontmatter.ts`'s `stripFencedBlocks` rather than fork it. D-23 makes the reader return a
verdict — `missing`, `unterminated`, `multiple` — so a malformed fence is one refusal both consumers
see identically, closing the live divergence 29-RESEARCH §A-6 reproduces verbatim in source. D-06
deletes the `^You` arm rather than supplementing it, because a predicate whose weakest arm already
dominates does not get a third arm.

Two more decide what the guard measures. D-07 makes it two-sided with both sides required; D-08
makes the PASS line carry the measurement rather than an assertion, which is the AP-1 fix stated as a
reporting rule. D-10 records the rejected alternative — a tokens-per-content-word ratio with a
threshold — so a later phase does not re-propose a tunable number.

D-38 supplies the sentence-identity function both this plan's uniqueness guard and plan 29-04's
disposition gate call. Clause-level segmentation is not a refinement here, it is the difference
between a guard that catches the defect and one that lands GREEN: exact sentence equality finds two
duplicate groups in the whole role corpus and zero in `software-engineer.md`, the file the phase
cites as its worked example. D-20 requires the de-duplication be enforced by a guard rather than
applied as a one-time pass, and D-21 keeps it intra-file so correct cross-file single-sourcing does
not trip it.

D-43 is why this plan does not stop at a RED transcript. Both arms of the voice predicate fail 17/17
independently at every N, so a guard that shipped the disjunction by mistake, or dropped an arm,
produces a byte-identical transcript. Three discriminating fixtures are the acceptance evidence; the
transcript alone is the strongest-looking and weakest-actual evidence in the phase.

Purpose: give the remaining twelve plans one fence authority, one vacuity rule, one sentence-identity
function, and two role-prose guards that have been watched failing against the real tree.
Output: two new modules with committed `.js` twins and tests, two rebuilt guards in the aggregator,
two RED transcripts, and six fixtures.

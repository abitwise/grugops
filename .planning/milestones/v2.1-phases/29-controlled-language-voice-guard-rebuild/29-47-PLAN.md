<objective>
Attack what this round built, on the axis this round introduced — then write down what was decided, what
was tried, and what remains.

Round 5's adversarial log has thirteen attempts and its own honest framing, and it still missed the
defect that mattered, because every attempt varied an INPUT and none varied the one degree of freedom the
round's own design decision had added. That is the transferable lesson and it is the whole reason for this
plan's first task: **name the degree of freedom the change introduces, then attack that.**

Deleting the conditional apparatus — both marker lists, the arm, and the type field — removes the verb axis
on BOTH groups and opens four others.

**Substring reach** — all three terms now match unconditionally, so they also match inside longer words and
inside text a reader would not call prose. **Term membership** — with no marker mechanism, the only thing
still enumerated is the pinned literal list itself, so a claim written with none of its members passes;
that is the residual this gate has always had and it must be stated plainly, with plants, rather than let a
deletion-shaped closure imply it away. **The widened corpus** — a document entered the scan this round, and
whether the file-scoped exemption and the pins behave correctly over it is a question nobody has asked.

And the fourth is now the sharpest. **The exemption region is the SOLE carve-out.** Before D-53 a legal
denial could survive either by sitting inside the region or by not carrying a marker on its line; the
second route is gone. Every sentence in the kit that names the standard or the topic in order to deny a
claim about it now depends entirely on the region's boundary being where the gate thinks it is. A boundary
defect that was a nuisance in round 5 is a correctness defect now, so region attacks are promoted from one
category among four to the category this pass spends the most attempts on: the first line, the last line,
one line either side, the heading itself, and a region that MOVES under an edit.

The deletion also moves the carried residuals, and mostly to zero. `V-29-42-01` (a claim split across a
hard wrap), `V-29-42-02` (a table row putting two cells on one line) and `V-29-42-04` (a marker inside an
HTML comment or a link target) are every one of them a property of a co-occurrence window that no longer
exists — so all three **close by construction**. That is a larger movement than the first authoring of this
plan anticipated, and it comes with a trap: "the mechanism is gone, so the residual is gone" is an
argument, and this repository does not accept an argument in place of a measurement. Each of the three gets
a closing measurement before it is marked closed. `V-29-42-03`'s single live instance is the ordinary-English
occurrence plan 29-44 rephrases, so it may go to zero too — and whether its sentence became TRUE or merely
VACUOUSLY true is a distinction that must be recorded rather than collapsed.

Purpose: a green suite is not proof for a safety invariant in this repository. The credible artifact is a
written list of what was tried and what happened — including the attempts that worked — and a reconciliation
that makes a silent drop impossible.

Output: the adversarial pass with one plant per reset mirror; `docs/audit/29-round6-residuals.md`; the
twelve-row reconciliation of round 5's findings; and the sweep.
</objective>

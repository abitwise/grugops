<objective>
Rewrite the second batch of eight roles onto the canonical skeleton, using the pipeline plan 29-05
proved, and take the phase from seven of seventeen clean to fifteen of seventeen.

The decisions are the same ones — D-19's section ownership, D-09's content removal from the caveman
block, D-41's rewritten opener, D-30's when-absent sentence, D-37's remove-before-add ordering, D-26's
untouchable ceiling table, D-47's generator order — and the reason this is a second plan rather than a
larger first one is context, not novelty. Fifteen role files rewritten sentence by sentence in one
agent's window is how a careful rewrite becomes a sloppy one at file eleven.

Two roles in this batch carry constraints the others do not. `incident-responder.md` is the single role
in the kit whose `## One job` is one sentence rather than two, so D-19's trailing-sentence delete frees
it exactly zero bytes; every byte it needs for the when-absent sentence has to come from the
caveman-block reduction and from compressing the first sentence. And `orchestrator.md` is the largest
role in the kit, sits 75 bytes from its WARN tier, and was already trimmed once in an earlier milestone
specifically so its ceiling would not be raised to accommodate growth — the same rule applies now, and
its `## One job` first sentence is additionally the routing description of the main-thread adapter, so
that one sentence has the widest blast radius in the batch.

Purpose: finish the general role corpus, leaving only the two dual-voice safety roles for a dedicated
plan.
Output: eight rewritten roles, their regenerated adapters, a regenerated catalog, and a disposition
file.
</objective>

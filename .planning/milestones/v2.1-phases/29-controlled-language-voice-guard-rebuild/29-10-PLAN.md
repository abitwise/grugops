<objective>
Apply the writing profile to the last six workflows — incident, UI design to build, the security audit,
context read and write, task claim, and context compaction — and take the workflow part of the governed
corpus to zero findings over its full denominator.

Three of these six are single-source documents the rest of the kit points at rather than restates. The
context read-write workflow is quoted by a pointer sentence in all seventeen roles and by the compaction
workflow; the compaction workflow is quoted by all seventeen too. That single-sourcing is the property
the kit's `## Hard limits` sections rely on when they say "this role references it and does not restate
it" — so a change to a sentence those pointers quote has to take the pointers with it in the same
commit, or the single-sourcing quietly becomes a paraphrase.

The context read-write workflow also holds the verify-before-write seam an earlier phase built. D-17
recorded that a style predicate deliberately does not go into that path. This plan conforms the
workflow's prose and changes nothing about the seam.

The security-audit and incident workflows carry safety-bearing clear-voice text, and the security-audit
workflow points at the generated OWASP checklist that D-42 excludes by marker. The checklist is not
rewritten; the workflow's own prose is, deliberately, one judgement at a time.

Purpose: finish the workflow corpus and produce the first full-denominator zero for the workflow part.
Output: six conforming workflows, a per-file growth record, a reconciled pointer set, and a disposition
file.
</objective>

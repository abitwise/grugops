<objective>
Close **WR-07** of `29-REVIEW.md` (round 4) and record this round's deferrals so the next verification pass
reads decisions rather than gaps.

**WR-07.** Seven census constants are pinned with exact equality over ALL test modules. Adding a single
assertion anywhere in the repository moves several of them. The property being defended — that no test
module carries two adjacent byte-identical assertions — depends on none of those values; they are
denominators. The consequence is behavioural rather than logical: the only way to clear the resulting red
is to bump the number, which is precisely the reflex this same round writes refusals against elsewhere in
the tree ("Do NOT widen the pin until it stops firing"; "LOWERING a count or NARROWING the arm are the two
ways to clear this finding by deleting what it measures"). A pin that fires on every unrelated commit trains
the maintainer to clear it without reading it, and no cost paragraph at the declaration says so.

**The deferrals.** Round 4 raised four informational findings — IN-01 (a scan-scope case asserting an
identity rather than a shortfall), IN-02 (the claim-heading recogniser matching every single-token
level-three heading), IN-03 (a third expression for the registry residue), IN-04 (`headingShapedFenced`
published and never asserted). The user decided this round's scope explicitly and placed all four OUT of it.
That decision has to live somewhere a verification round will read it, or the next round records four
silent drops.

Purpose: per D-25 an exact number is legitimate where it is a measurement that fails closed and cannot
drift; a live census over a growing corpus is neither. Per D-04 a companion pinned to a moving range
disarms itself, so the snapshot belongs against a fixed commit. Per D-08 every surviving number keeps a
witness or an honest statement that it has none.

Output: a census that reds when the classifier breaks and stays quiet when a test is added, and a committed
residual record carrying the four deferrals, the carried residual roll-up and this round's probe-coverage
arithmetic.
</objective>

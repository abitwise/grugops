<objective>
Close the round-3 review's WR-04, IN-02, and the recorded variant C1 / residual T-29-23-05 that share
one root: the safety EXEMPTION in `guard_banned_claims` is located through the shared authority, and
nothing downstream checks the answer or measures its size.

**WR-04.** Before plan 29-23 the heading index came out of the same array the count was built from, so
"count is exactly 1" made "index is valid" true by construction. It is now TWO separate traversals with
two separately-spelled predicates: the COUNT walks the caller's `lines`, the LOCATE walks `text`, and
`sectionEndIndex(text, headingAt + 1, 2)` at `:548` is NOT guarded on `-1`. They agree today only
because `text === lines.join("\n")`. If either drifts — a caller splitting on a different newline
pattern, a normalisation change in one place — `headingAt` is `-1`, the body slice starts at line 0,
and the scan's exemption test is TRUE FOR EVERY LINE FROM ZERO. That is a fail-open widening of a
safety exemption reached through a `-1` nobody checked, and it is the one site of the pattern in this
tree that is not guarded.

**Variant C1 / T-29-23-05.** An unterminated fence opened inside the exemption region moves `endBefore`
and swallows the appended section into the exemption. It was dismissed in round 2's own transcript as
"Nothing NEW" because the occurrence pin reds when the swallowed text carries a banned claim — but the
pin measures OCCURRENCES, not EXTENT, so swallowing a section that carries none is still silent. Round
3's review says plainly that CR-01 is adjacent to C1 and that the difference is that the voice guards
had no equivalent pin. LANG-06 got one in plan 29-27. This module has not.

Purpose: LANG-07 is about ONE parser over the bytes. An authority is only one authority if its contract
— including what `-1` means — is honoured by every consumer. A consumer that reads the answer without
checking it has quietly reintroduced a second behaviour.

Output: the `-1` guarded at the live site and asserted as a CLASS tree-wide, the drift route deleted
rather than defended, and the exemption's extent published and pinned.
</objective>

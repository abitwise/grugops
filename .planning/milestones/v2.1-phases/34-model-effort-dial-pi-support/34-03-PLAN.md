<objective>
Make the effort resolution visible and checked. The generator announces what effort it resolved, in
the same anchored grammar the model announcements use, and the freshness gate refuses a mirrored
run whose effort resolution is not the zero-config one — so the gate can never certify a configured
regeneration as the committed output (the CR-01 defect, RESEARCH Pitfall 3; D-06). Then give every
TIERED row its required effort rationale and pin the D-04 derived split and the D-05 independence of
the two presets.

Purpose: the D-06 byte-identity guarantee is only as good as the gate that checks it; a gate that
reads only the model half is half-blind. The discretion item "announce the effort resolution" is
taken: yes, through the one grammar adapters-freshness already reads.

Output: effort announcement grammar and readers, generator announcement lines, freshness assertion,
per-role effort rationale, and tests.

Spec-less probe fallback: no SPEC.md; edge cases come from D-04, D-05, D-06 and RESEARCH Pitfall 3
and Pattern E4.
</objective>

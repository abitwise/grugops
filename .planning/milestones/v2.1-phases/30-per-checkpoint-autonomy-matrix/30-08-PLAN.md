Complete the disposition set: the non-blocking tier allows the action, records a finding in the trace
and names the checkpoint in the banner; the unauthorized-lowering case records its own finding; and the
zero-config differential is finalized against the full roster rather than the tracer's two ids.

Purpose: the tracer proved the blocking path. The non-blocking path is where a lowering becomes
invisible if it is only a message on standard error — and invisibility is precisely what AUTO-05 exists
to prevent.

Output: a notify branch that writes through the one sanctioned note path, a banner produced from the
same evaluation as the decision, and a whole-run differential that stays at exactly one added line with
every checkpoint in the roster.
</objective>

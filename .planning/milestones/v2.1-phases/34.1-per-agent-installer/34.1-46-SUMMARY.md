# Phase 34.1 Plan 46: the reader-state matrix, the remaining follow rows and the widened removal census Summary

**D-37 is now tested over the reader's whole state space rather than over cases found by earlier reviews. A matrix keyed by `MarkerDefect` covers 21 hand-edited marker shapes under the five runs that print about the marker, 105 cells in all. In every cell the run names the cause and the by-hand fix and never offers removing the marker. It removes nothing, leaves the marker path as it was, and prints no control byte. R47 (mode 000) and R48a-e follow the remaining reader states as printed until the uninstall finishes. A widened removal matcher asked of every printed reading finds zero offers to delete the marker, and three planted offers each turn it red. No product file needed a change.**

    - retired-record 1: `createdFiles`
    - ledger 2: one entry's kind banana, `ledger` "x"
  - **Runs:** `PRINTING_RUNS` is `--check`, `install --claude`, `install --codex`, a flag-less uninstall and `uninstall --codex`.
  - **Table checks:** a test asserts that the table's keys equal MARKER_DEFECTS (8), that every defect has at least one shape, and that there are 21 shapes and 5 runs.
  - **Per-cell assertions:** each cell asserts the exit code (1 or 3) and finds a line that names `.grugops/install.json` with `by hand`, `never delete the marker` and every entry of `markerRemedy(read).fix`. It also asserts:
    - no removal offer

- **The widened removal-matcher census (Task 3):**
  - **What it asks:** `MARKER_REMOVAL_ANY` (delete, remove and erase, with their -s and -ing forms, or an `rm` command) runs over every reading of every maximal string expression in the nine product modules. There is no TOUCH preselection. Each hit is read as its verb plus the object up to the end of the clause.
  - **What counts as an offer:** a hit is an offer when it is not negated and its object is the marker. Negated means never, not, nor, cannot, nothing or no just before the verb, or "nothing" in the object. The marker counts as the object when it is named, or when the object is a pronoun or "the file" in a reading or sentence whose subject is the marker.

It sees exactly two hits whose object is the marker, the owner's "never delete the marker" and keepWithoutRecord's "never delete ${marker}", and both are negated.

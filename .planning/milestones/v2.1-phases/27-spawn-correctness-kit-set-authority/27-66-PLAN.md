asserted rather than intended.

through 11 each REPAIRED a defect in `scripts/frontmatter.ts`, and rounds 10 and 11 each shipped a

and CR-02 are therefore DISSOLVED, not FIXED, and a register row that says FIXED would tell a later
reader something false about the state of that module.

its own arithmetic. Output: the round-12 register and the round-12 traceability hold, both in
`deferred-items.md`.

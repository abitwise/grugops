# v3.0 deferred-item triage

At the v3.0 close the GSD open-artifact audit listed 768 deferred items. They come from the `deferred-items.md` files of v3.0 phases 36–43 and of the archived v2.1 phases. Four agents checked every item against the code at HEAD `99fc93ee`, not against the time it was logged. Each item got a verdict, a category, a priority and a root-cause theme. The per-item verdicts and evidence are in `v3.0-deferred-triage.jsonl`.

Priorities:
- **P1:** a user-facing problem or a broken gate. That means a false claim in shipped docs or roles, red CI, a safety gap, or a defect in a shipped tool.
- **P2:** a real but contained problem.
- **P3:** cosmetic.

<objective>
Give a floor conflict found at bootstrap a path that can run (gap G-45-9b, review finding WR-06; the human ruled "fix WR-06 WR-07").

Plan 45-14 made Workflow 00 step 1 say "apply no decision that conflicts with a floor" and pointed at Workflow 20 for what happens instead. The Workflow 20 floor path is ticket-shaped: BA/PM files per `## Build requests` steps 1 to 5 and puts both bullets on each affected ticket. At Workflow 00 step 1 no ticket exists (tickets come at step 6), and the build-request contract needs at least one ticket in `## Blocks`. Workflow 20's provenance bullet limits the Greenfield Mapper to recording which source and pin gave each item, its role `## Output` names no observation, and Workflow 00 has no floor stop. So bootstrap either stalls on a refused file or continues on `default_stack` with no stop and no record.

The fix follows the review's own proposal, with Workflow 20 kept as the single statement of the floor rule:
- Workflow 00 step 1: the Mapper applies no conflicting decision, leaves that stack item `UNKNOWN - verify` (not filled from `default_stack`), and reports the decision and its floor to the Orchestrator, which routes the conflict per Workflow 20.
- Workflow 20 floor path: the Orchestrator routes a conflict the Mapper reports to the Architect, who records the observation (the same route the Orchestrator's own findings already take). A new last bullet says what changes at bootstrap: BA/PM files at Workflow 00 step 6, naming each ticket that needs the item, and writes that ticket first when none does. Workflow 00 waits before step 7 until a named human lifts the stop.
- Workflow 00 `## Stop conditions`: one new untagged bullet, so WORKFLOW_STOP_BULLET_COUNT moves 58 -> 59.
- Workflow 20's provenance bullet stops limiting the Mapper to provenance.

Planner mechanism choices (recorded so a reviewer can reverse them):
- The Mapper reports the conflict and keeps working. It does not mark its subtask `BLOCKED`. In this kit, "report `BLOCKED`" ends a role's subtask and hands the work on, which would leave steps 2 to 6 undone. Then no ticket would exist, the request could never be filed, and the stop would again come before the filing (the G-45-1 shape). The stop that the gap truth asks for is the Workflow 00 stop bullet, placed after the filing.
- The Mapper records no observation. Its role file is not edited: role edits are one pointer line each (Phase 40 D-05, D-18), and the role byte budgets are tight (see plan 45-14). The Architect, whose `## Output` allows observation notes, records the observation on the Orchestrator's routing. This also closes the gap artifact "greenfield-mapper.md `## Output` names no observation note".
- The stop comes right after the filing at step 6 ("before step 7"). That is earlier than the review's "before step 8 sets the structure", and it matches Workflow 20's "the Orchestrator confirms both bullets, then stops".

Out of scope (not ruled by the human): WR-04 (step 1 writes the provenance record before step 2 drafts greenfield-plan.md), WR-08, WR-09 and every IN finding. WR-07 is plan 45-16.

Purpose: a floor conflict at bootstrap ends in a filed request on a real ticket, a human-stop bullet and a stop before any structure is set. No upstream decision moves a floor.
Output: Workflow 00 step 1 and stop bullet, the Workflow 20 bootstrap bullet and provenance line, the stop count 59 with rebuilt `.js` and hook manifest, the text tests, the disposition rows, and WR-06 recorded as fixed.
</objective>

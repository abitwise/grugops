Decision 3, text fix only: no new program check (the 45-10 decision and accepted risk T-45-35 stay). Review finding WR-03 (step 12 ignores a ticket's other blockers) is adopted here because the floor-stop bullet depends on it.

How a deviation or floor-conflict request is told apart (planner's mechanism under decision 1): a request file has no field for it (D-05 closed key set), so step 4 makes BA/PM say so in `## Question` when the observation records a deviation or a floor conflict, and step 13 and the stop bullet reserve the withdrawal of such a request to a named human.

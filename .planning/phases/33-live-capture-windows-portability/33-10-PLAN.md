<objective>
Spend the phase's one authorized live run, and come back with an artifact rather than an opinion.

Everything that can be proven at zero tokens has been: the predicates are red-and-green tested
offline against a committed fixture, the runner walks every phase under a dry run, the lane is a
wrapper, and both CI legs have been measured. What remains is the observation itself — CAP-03's
whole premise is that the spawn fix is proven by a captured run and not by a green suite, which is
exactly what failed to detect the original defect.

The budget discipline is locked. One live run per gap-closure round, behind its own blocking
checkpoint that states the measured spend floor; the dry run exits 0 and reports ready first; a red
run is diagnosed at zero tokens and re-asked in the NEXT round.

Purpose: turn the milestone's headline claim into a committed, citable artifact.
Output: a redacted transcript, a derived summary with one outcome line, and — if the run is red —
a zero-token diagnosis naming its cause class and the observation that would distinguish it from
the next.
</objective>

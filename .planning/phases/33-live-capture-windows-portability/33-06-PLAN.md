<objective>
Close the last three Windows failure clusters that belong to nobody else: the installer suite's
temporary-directory and line-ending expectations, the temporary-clone rebuild, and the precheck
script's own regex anchor.

Two of these are cheap and measured. The third — eight failures where the rebuild inside a
temporary clone exits non-zero — is the one item CAP-02 names by hand ("temp-dir tsc mirror
rebuild") and the one RESEARCH could not confirm: assumption A4 says the failure is the compiler
inside the clone rather than the clone itself, and the compiler's own error text was never in the
log. RESEARCH Open Question 5 offers a cheaper hypothesis first — the eight may be downstream of
the absent scratch root that plan 33-02 already fixed.

Purpose: diagnose before fixing, so the boundary chosen is the boundary that failed.
Output: canonical temporary-path comparisons, a line-ending-agnostic expectation, a rebuild failure
that quotes the compiler, and a precheck anchor that matches what the script prints.
</objective>

THE HOLE IS EXACTLY THE SPELLINGS BEGINNING WITH "/" THAT ARE NOT "./" OR "../". One predicate is
defined as "not '.' and not '/'", the other as "starts with '.'". The "/" prefix is subtracted by
the first and never added by the second, so it is owned by neither. That is a complement, not a
partition, and it is the precise shape the round's canonical-form cutover proposes to replace.

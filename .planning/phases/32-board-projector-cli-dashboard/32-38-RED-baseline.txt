   attempt to reach it" rule)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A tree carrying all four populations at once — a self-declaring document, a contest winner, a
contest loser, a lone mismatched document, a grammar-refused document, and the degenerate `.md`
entry — was read with `readTicketsSource` and indexed with `ticketPopulations`:

No input the reader can produce reaches B3. The reason is structural rather than incidental:
`ticketPopulations` fills `byStem` and `byId` in ONE loop over the SAME record list, so every value
in `byStem` is some record's `id` and every record's `id` is a key of `byId` (first-wins changes
WHICH record a key holds, never WHETHER the key is present). Task 2 records the outcome and states
the invariant as a derived assertion rather than deleting the branch on the strength of an argument.

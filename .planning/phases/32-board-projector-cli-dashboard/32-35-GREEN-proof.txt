THE LATENCIES DID NOT MOVE, AND THEY WERE NOT SUPPOSED TO. Nothing in the watch path changed. What
changed is how long the wait is willing to sit before giving up, which is the difference between a
red that prints `the change took 812 ms, which is at or past the 1000 ms poll period` and a red that
prints a runner-level timeout with no number in it at all.

The poll path, the watch record and the debounce were re-measured in the same six runs and every one
is within noise of its before value: poll 998/997/992 → 1002/995/996 ms; record published
276/275/275 → 275/276/274 ms and cleared 897/892/901 → 894/899/897 ms; debounce coalesced five
writes into one document in all six.

Thirty-nine lines, byte-identical. The two comparisons that decide attribution —

THE NEW CASE PROVES THE DEADLINE STILL BOUNDS SOMETHING. It asserts the two inequalities rather than

a case that can only ever red. Both are silent failures of a derived constant, and both now red
here. Pinning 750 instead would restate the arithmetic one line above it and prove nothing else.

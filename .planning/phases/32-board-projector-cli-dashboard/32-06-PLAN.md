<objective>
Prove mechanically that the dashboard cannot write, and that it cannot open a socket.

Purpose: this is the requirement that makes grugops's read-only stance structural instead of
declared. Every comparable tool is a control plane; grugops's projector is deliberately not one, and
CLAUDE.md's hard rule is that such a property is enforced by a mechanism rather than by a sentence in
a docblock. Research also found the two traps this guard would otherwise fall into: a runtime-derived
count pinned across two Node majors reds CI for a reason unrelated to the dashboard, and a
stdlib-only second scanner would give one predicate two authorities — the exact Phase 29 failure.

Output: `scripts/board-readonly.test.ts` with the derived sets, the premise assertions, the module
ban and the discrimination proof, plus the `check:dashboard-readonly` npm script.

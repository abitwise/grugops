additive" and "Any shape change bumps this number and updates the golden in the same commit" — and
resolving it is part of this decision rather than a detail underneath it.

`32-15` faced the same question one population over and answered it by adding `unadmittedTickets` to
`JoinInputs` rather than to the snapshot, with the reason written at the type.

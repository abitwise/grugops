§ 4 — THE DUPLICATE-IDENTIFIER WALK'S COST SHAPE (WR-08)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

`scripts/board-read.ts:1303` runs `records.find((r) => r.id === id)` once per admitted entry,
over the records accumulated so far — a linear scan inside the walk, so n(n-1)/2 comparisons at
n admitted entries. At the walk bound (MAX_WALK_ENTRIES = 10000, `scripts/kit-model.ts:205`) that
is ~50,000,000 comparisons on every refresh, and the refresh runs on every watch event and every
poll tick.

Method: a copy of the fixture whose `plans/tickets/` carries N synthetic admitted documents
(each `id: TKT-nnnnnn`, unique, so the scan never short-circuits early). One warm-up call, then
three timed `readSnapshot(root)` calls; the best of three is reported, all three are shown.

| Entries under plans/tickets/ | Admitted | `readSnapshot` best of 3 | all three runs (ms)  | ratio vs previous size |
|------------------------------|----------|--------------------------|----------------------|------------------------|
| 2,507                        | 2,500    | **92.3 ms**              | 95.6, 94.6, 92.3     | —                      |
| 5,007                        | 5,000    | **212.5 ms**             | 212.7, 212.5, 236.7  | 2.30x for 2x the input |
| 10,007 (bound bites at 10,000)| 10,000  | **510.5 ms**             | 659.1, 510.5, 511.4  | 2.40x for 2x the input |

The measured growth is SUPERLINEAR and the superlinear term is the scan: doubling the input costs
2.3-2.4x rather than 2x. It is not a clean n^2 curve because per-file I/O and parsing dominate the
absolute number at these sizes; the scan is the part that grows faster than the input, and it is
the only part of this loop that does.

THE NUMBER TASK 4 RE-RUNS: **510.5 ms best-of-3 at 10,000 admitted entries**, same machine, same
three-run method, same copy layout.

The tickets source at 10,007 entries reads `stale` with the bound reported, which is D-14's stated
behaviour (a bounded listing is a stale badge, never a frozen screen) and is unchanged by this
plan.

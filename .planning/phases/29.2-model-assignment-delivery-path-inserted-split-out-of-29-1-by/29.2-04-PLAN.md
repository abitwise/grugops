<objective>
Bound what `dest` can be before the installer writes through it.

`materializeAdapter()` reads and `writeFileSync`s a target adapter destination without ever
asking what that destination is. On the ordinary re-run — the re-run D-07 and D-15 made the
documented, routine way to deliver a `models` edit — that destroys or creates files anywhere on
disk, at exit 0, with no warning. The `--check` doctor (D-09) has the same blind spot on its read
side. This plan closes VERIFICATION.md truth 10 and REVIEW.md CR-01.

**Purpose:** the phase's D-13 says an installed adapter is a kit-owned derived artifact and may be
rewritten. That claim is true of a regular file inside the target and false of everything else. This
plan is the bound on D-13, not a retreat from it. D-11's skip-if-identical arm sits directly behind
the unguarded read, so the refusal must precede it.

**Output:** one hazard predicate with two consumers, a hard refusal in the agents write loop, and
six RED-first regression cases covering the measured shapes.

**Two facts that will break a naive fix, both measured this session:**

1. `realpathSync(mkdtempSync(tmpdir()))` differs from its own input on macOS
   (`/var/folders/…` → `/private/var/folders/…`). Every fixture in `install.test.ts` builds its
   target that way. A containment check that compares a raw `TARGET` against a resolved `dest`
   refuses **every install in the suite**. Resolve both sides.
2. `if (CHECK) { process.exit(doctor()); }` runs at char 46571 of the committed `install.js`;
   `const isSymlink` is initialized at char 51728. A predicate the doctor calls **must** be
   declared above that early exit or it throws a temporal-dead-zone `ReferenceError` on every
   `--check` run. `install.ts` already documents this rule above `verify()`.

Decisions this plan touches: D-07, D-09, D-11, D-13, D-15.

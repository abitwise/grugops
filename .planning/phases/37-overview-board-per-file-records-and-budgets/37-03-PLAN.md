<objective>
Build the one mechanism that lets a single-file, builtins-only host runnable use the board grammar, the non-blocking
reader, the budget rules and the budget numbers without a second copy of any of them, and ship the first runnable on
it: `tools/grugops/state-check.js` (SPLIT-05).

Per the carried-forward Phase 36 rule, budget numbers reach host runnables by generation into a marked region plus a
freshness gate (the `scripts/generate-hook-manifest.ts` pattern), never by hand copy. Research measured every needed
closure as import-free or `node:`-only, so a generator over the TypeScript syntax tree can embed declarations verbatim.
Task 1 is the tracer: generator, gate, CI wiring and a state-check that measures the board budget with marker severity
(D-04, D-05), proven by running the committed `.js` alone in an empty directory. Task 2 brings every other budget (D-10)
into state-check, adds `--json`, output sanitization and the read-only proof, under the D-12 exit contract.

Purpose: SPLIT-05's "budgets generated from the layout module, freshness-gated"; index and trace integrity follow in
plans 37-04 and 37-06. Output: the generator and its gate, the state-check runnable and its tests, amended censuses.
</objective>

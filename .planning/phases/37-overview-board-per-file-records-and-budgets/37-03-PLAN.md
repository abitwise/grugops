<objective>
Build the one mechanism that lets a single-file, builtins-only host runnable use the board grammar, the non-blocking
reader, the budget rules and the budget numbers without a second copy of any of them, and ship the first runnable on
it: `tools/grugops/state-check.js` measuring the board budget (SPLIT-05).

Per the carried-forward Phase 36 rule, budget numbers reach host runnables by generation into a marked region plus a
freshness gate (the `scripts/generate-hook-manifest.ts` pattern), never by hand copy. Research measured every needed
closure as import-free or `node:`-only, so a generator over the TypeScript syntax tree can embed declarations verbatim.
The single task is the tracer: generator, gate, CI wiring and a state-check that measures the board budget with marker
severity (D-04, D-05) under the D-12 exit contract, proven by running the committed `.js` alone in an empty directory.

This plan is the first half of one slice. On 2026-10-21 the slice was split at its task boundary to keep each plan inside
the smart-zone budget: plan 37-04 brings every other D-10 budget into state-check and adds `--json`, output sanitization
and the read-only proof. Index and trace integrity follow in plans 37-05 and 37-09.

Purpose: SPLIT-05's "budgets generated from the layout module, freshness-gated". Output: the generator and its gate,
the state-check runnable and its tests, amended censuses.
</objective>

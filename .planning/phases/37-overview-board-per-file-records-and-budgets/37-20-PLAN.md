  reaches each runnable's last statement. Asynchronous EPIPE on a closed pipe is not modeled (it is not a throw).
- executeRoll (scripts/state-rules.ts lines 1078-1135 at d9fa837c) catches every throw from its re-reads, writes, proof

  program under test keeps its own default.

<objective>
Close BLOCKER 1 and PARTIAL A of round-4 verification: the D-08.1 exemption's unit mismatch, and the
two path spellings entering one compared set.

**One root cause, three shipped spellings.** `exemptMentions += named.length` counts CAPTURES of
`CONFIG_NAMED_PATH`; `grepSubstring` greps OCCURRENCES of `CONFIG_REF_NEEDLE`. Two units, one
published number. The consequence is not a rounding error — it is that an occurrence which yields no
capture, or whose capture is a prefix of a longer path, is absorbed into an already-exempt line
without the membership predicate ever being asked about it. Round 4 reproduced three spellings of
this against the committed `.js` on a hermetic mirror, all three at TRUE exit 0 with all three
published cardinalities exactly as declared, one of them shipping an actionable instruction naming a
private-key path.

**The fix this plan takes is the change of unit, not a third character class.** Rounds 3 and 4 both
answered "is this capture admissible" and neither asked "which occurrences is the predicate even
asked about". So the extraction is rewritten to emit exactly one record per needle occurrence — the
same needle `grepSubstring` greps — and the published number becomes that record count by
construction. The membership question is then asked of every occurrence, because there is no
occurrence without a record. `CONFIG_NAMED_PATH` is deleted rather than widened: this repository's
recorded terminal lesson is one format-aware authority per predicate and the deletion of the second
grammar, and a widened character class is the same heuristic wearing a bigger alphabet.

**And the gate asserts its own premise.** The unit mismatch was invisible for two rounds because
nothing in the gate compared the two numbers. It now does, on every hit, and prints both when they
disagree. That assertion is worthless on the day it is written — the two units are identical by
construction — and it is the thing that catches the next refactor that makes them differ again.

**PARTIAL A rides along because it is the same sentence.** `ghLegal.add(PACKAGING_TEMPLATE)` takes a
raw forward-slash literal into a set compared against `walk()` output. The comment forty lines above
already states the correct reasoning — `join()` keeps these byte-identical to the paths `walk()`
produces on Windows as well as Unix — and the reasoning is right while its application is
incomplete. One spelling authority for every path in every compared set.

Purpose: the exemption's declared number describes the tree it judges, and the gate that publishes
it can no longer certify text it never looked at.
Output: a rewritten extraction and membership path in `scripts/check-kit-refs.ts` with its committed
`.js`, four new discriminating oracle cases plus a `path.win32` unit case, and the ledger rows this
change closes and opens.
</objective>

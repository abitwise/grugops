## Part 2 — The placement, and why it is not where the plan's prose said

The plan's action says to author the claim *"inside the named honesty region"*. Its key link says
the statement *"must be placed and worded with that region's boundary derived, not guessed"*. The
derivation and the prose disagree, and the derivation wins — that is what deriving a boundary is
for.

**What placing it inside would have cost.** The region ends at end of file, so any line inserted
into it moves `endBefore` while `headingAt` stays put:

- `BANNED_CLAIM_EXEMPT_EXTENT` = 75 would have gone to 88 and red the gate.
- `BANNED_CLAIM_EXEMPT_ANCHORS` = 6 would have gone to 7 and red the gate.

Both pins live in `scripts/check-banned-claims.ts` — **the exact file this task's own acceptance
criteria require to show an empty diff**, and the file D-16 exists to keep untouched. Placing the
claim inside the region would have forced two edits to it.

**What placing it above the region costs: nothing.** A section inserted *before* `## Disclaimer and
honesty floor` shifts `headingAt` and `endBefore` by the same amount, so the extent is arithmetically
invariant. Measured after the edit:

```
region: {"headingAt":247,"endBefore":322}   extent: 75 (pinned 75)
```

**And it is the STRONGER position, which is why this is not a workaround.** Outside the region the
claim's bytes are **SCANNED** by `guard_banned_claims` rather than exempted from it. The carve-out
exists because a disclaimer must be able to name the standard in order to deny affiliation with it.
This claim needs to name no banned topic to make its denial, so it earns no carve-out and takes
none. That reasoning is recorded in the registry row itself, not only here.

**Recorded as a deviation** (Rule 3 — blocking) in the deviations section below.

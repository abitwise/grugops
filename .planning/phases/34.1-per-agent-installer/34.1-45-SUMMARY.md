# Phase 34.1 Plan 45: the siblings beside the owner (IN-34, WR-26) and the unusable-marker census Summary

**Over a marker the uninstaller cannot use, the ask-rules and CLAUDE.md block reports now point at the marker's verify line instead of telling the user to remove the grugops ask rules or block lines by hand; following the one printed remedy reverses them by record (R45).

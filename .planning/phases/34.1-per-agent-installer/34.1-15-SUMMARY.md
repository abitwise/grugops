## Decisions Made

See key-decisions. Every same-run command row in (f) records how its fragment reaches a counted verify, and the parser
asserts that path. A row reaches the verify through one of these:
- a direct `verify(` call;
- `refusals.push` in buildKitPlan, whose refusals the kit step prints with `for (const why of kitPlan.refusals) verify(why)`;
- a `report("kit", ...)` right after that loop or after `for (const why of go.verifies) verify(why)`;
- a reason built in adapterDestHazard, kitDestDecision or kitWriteDecision, whose result reaches those loops.

The plan asked for the stricter check "the call is `verify(`". These sites print through the loops listed above, so the
check follows those loops.

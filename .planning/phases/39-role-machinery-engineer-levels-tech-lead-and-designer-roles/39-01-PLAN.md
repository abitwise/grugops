<objective>
Build the one derivation every later Phase 39 plan reads: which agent adapters exist (one per role, or one per declared level),
and what model and effort each one resolves to (TIER-02, TIER-06; D-13, D-14, D-15, D-16).

Today every consumer assumes one role stem, one adapter name and one model resolution. This plan adds the derivation and the
per-adapter resolution as pure library functions and proves them on hermetic mirrors. Nothing on the live tree changes
behavior: no role declares `levels:` yet, so the derived names equal today's 17 adapters byte for byte. Plan 39-02 switches the
consumers; plan 39-07 turns the levels on.

Purpose: one authority for adapter identity and per-adapter assignment, so no guard, generator or gate can answer differently.
Output: kit-model and model-tiers additions with their tests, the board-readonly closure row and the regenerated hook manifest.
</objective>

# Phase 39 Plan 08: The Tech Lead in the Ticket Flow and the Escalation Path Summary

**WF04 now goes Definition of Ready, then the Tech Lead's score and plan, then the exact engineer level the plan names. The engineer is spawned by name as a non-fork sub-agent, with no `model` or `effort` parameter. When an engineer is blocked, or its gate fails for a reason an agent can fix, it publishes a `failed-attempt` note and moves the ticket to `Blocked`. The Tech Lead then picks one move: it can escalate one level, at most once per ticket. The Orchestrator re-enqueues a new `pending/` subtask. A named human ends the path, and the WF05 self-fix human stop is kept. orchestrator.md routes the Tech Lead and the Designer and shrank from 7,359 to 7,255 bytes.**

<objective>
Let a role declare `skills:` in its frontmatter under the canonical frontmatter grammar, with a parser, a guard and a
fixture, while keeping it out of every generated adapter (TIER-07; D-28, D-29).

The research found that the canonical grammar refuses flow lists (`[a, b]`) and refuses `:` in a plain scalar, so the list
form is space-separated kebab tokens, the same as `capabilities`. Instead of widening the adapter schema the research
proposed, this plan adds a second key set, ROLE_SCHEMA, admitted through the same grammar by a new admitRole() entry point.
CANONICAL_SCHEMA and admit() stay byte-unchanged, so the adapter safety authority does not move at all and an adapter
carrying `skills:` is still refused. Phase 42 fills in values after its evaluation; no shipped role declares one here.

Purpose: Phase 42's doctor and Phase 43's docs read a declaration that already has one grammar and one parser.
Output: ROLE_SCHEMA, admitRole, roleSkillsOf, guard_role_frontmatter, the fixture and the proof cases.
</objective>

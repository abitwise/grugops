<objective>
Close UAT gap G-39-71 (verification finding W1) by implementing the human's binding decision of 2026-10-31
(Olger Oeselg, 39-UAT.md test 71), recorded here as **D-21 amended**: when the Tech Lead cannot resolve a
blocked ticket, it hands the ticket to the role that can fix the cause (BA/PM, the Architect, or another suitable
role); a named human is asked only after the roles have argued three times without resolution, or when the fix
needs a fundamental change to requirements or rules. "Human's role is stakeholder."

Today the used-up stop and the two-level re-score stop send the ticket straight to a named human at `block`, and
at `notify`/`off` the step and the tagged stop disagree (W1). This plan replaces both with one hand-off loop that
runs at every value of `checkpoints.escalate_unadjudicable_result`. The cell then decides only the final human
stop: at `block` a named human decides; at `notify`/`off` the ticket waits in `Blocked` with no move, which is the
human's 2026-10-30 park-in-blocked choice (39-18) carried to the one point where it still applies. The same rewrite
closes W2 (exit from the parked state, a lowered stale-blocker cell, a one-time observation), W3 (one human stop
with a resume record and a step 16 hold, "never escalate twice on your own", no limit counted for a human's move)
and W4 (the human sees the move note before confirming it).

Decisions honored: D-21 amended (this plan records it), D-19 (the Tech Lead decides every move), D-20 (no relayed
data: the receiving role is dispatched through the queue with the task reference only), D-21's no-loop half (two
moves plus at most three hand-offs, then the human stop), D-05 (the Architect owns design), D-17 (named dispatch).
Out of scope by the human's deferral: the same rule for the kit's other checkpoints (Workflow 16's disagreement
stop and the rest).

Purpose: the human is the stakeholder, asked only for a real disagreement or a fundamental change; agents never
loop and never settle a requirement or rule change on their own.
Output: the decision record, the Workflow 04 hand-off loop (steps 15-17 and the used-up stop), the same rule in
tech-lead.md and factory.config.md, the BA/PM and Architect pointers, the test pins and the disposition rows.

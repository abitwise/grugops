<objective>
Close **WR-01** and **WR-04** together at both walk sites. They are one surface and one argument: the
D-29 ancestor stack answers *membership* correctly and answers *cost* not at all, and the arm where
it stops descending says nothing at all.

WR-01 measured 131,072 members and 51.3 seconds from 18 directories and 153 symlinks with **no cycle
present**, doubling per added directory. `scripts/kit-model.ts` carries the identical shape and runs
inside `check-foundation-guards.js` in CI, so such a tree **hangs the gate rather than failing it** —
and the installer walks whatever the source-root variable points at, which is a user-supplied root.

WR-04 is the honesty half: the cycle arm returns with no report, no name and no verification line,
against both modules' twice-stated invariant that a member they cannot see is a member they cannot
refuse by name.

Purpose: keep the two concerns SEPARATE. Conflating "is this a cycle on this path" with "how much
work may this walk do" produced the old global-visited-set defect and then produced this one.

Output: two walks with a per-path cycle answer and a per-walk work bound, each surfacing its refusal
through that side's documented floor — reported in the installer, thrown in the authority — never
silently.

## Decisions this plan implements

- **D-31** — round 4 closes all eight round-3 findings; this plan owns WR-01 and WR-04.
- **D-35 (NEW)** — the work bound is a SEPARATE, explicit mechanism from the cycle answer. The
  ancestor stack stays per-path and answers membership; a per-walk counter answers cost. The bound is
  a named constant `MAX_WALK_ENTRIES` set to 10000 at both sites. Exceeding it is a reported refusal
  in the installer (a verification finding, so the run reports incomplete rather than claiming
  completion) and a thrown named error in the kit-set authority. Never a silent truncation.
  Reversibility: **reversible**.
- **D-36 (NEW, AMENDS D-29's kit-model half)** — the cycle arm becomes a REPORTED event. The
  installer pushes the relative path it declined to descend into and the caller surfaces it as a
  verification finding naming that path; the kit-set authority THROWS a named error carrying the same
  relative path, matching its throw-not-report floor and its other fail-closed floors. This
  deliberately amends the shipped kit-model cycle case, which today asserts that a cycle "yields the
  REAL member set" — under D-36 it must assert a named throw instead. That amendment is intentional
  and must not be reversed by weakening the throw to make the old case pass. Honest scoping recorded
  with it: because both sites drop the same set, this does not violate the blind-to-a-member
  invariant and install/uninstall stay symmetric, so it is strictly weaker than CR-03, and the
  platform-loading premise carries `UNKNOWN - verify`. Reversibility: **costly** — it changes the
  behavior of a CI authority and amends a locked decision.

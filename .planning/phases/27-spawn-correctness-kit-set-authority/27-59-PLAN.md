<objective>
Make the harness able to see what it claims completeness over, **before** anything this round is
called closed. This is the phase's own most-repeated lesson, stated in its record five separate times:
*a corpus that cannot generate an input cannot prove the fix that input needed.* Round 10 shipped
three live bypasses past a 210-green file, a green foundation gate and a green 312-cell loader
differential.

Two things happen here.

**The D-52 corpus grows over a DERIVED axis.** The key-line axis must be able to express the three
round-11 critical families — a block-owned region beside a quoted region under one key, a nested key
spelling outside the top-level charset, and a node property standing in front of a block indicator.
It grows the way `deriveKeyLines` already grows it: from the base shapes' own declared fields, so a
family nobody has thought of yet is reachable by construction rather than by a future author
remembering to add a row. Adding three hand-written rows for the three reported families would be
circular over the family structure exactly as a per-arm corpus was circular over the arm structure.

**WR-03 — the state differential's two inputs stop being one.** The loop passes one variable as BOTH
the entering node-may-begin state and the offset-zero node-start fact. `stripComment`'s own doc block
insists these are not the same fact, and the live continuation call site routinely produces the
combination where the entering state is false and the line-start answer is true. That combination is
never generated: the vector space is 24 where it should be 48. The case therefore reproduces exactly
the trap its sibling names — *"not because the module agreed with the loader but because IT NEVER
GENERATED THE INPUT"* — inside the fix for it.

Purpose: this plan produces the evidence every closure claim in `27-61` will rest on. It runs AFTER
the four source fixes so the never-exemptible directions can legitimately be empty, and BEFORE the
disposition register so no family is declared closed on a corpus that could not have seen it.

Output: a derived corpus that expresses all three families, proven non-circular against a
pre-round-11 mirror and proven able to fail against each individual revert; a decoupled state axis
pinned at its full product with a regenerated capture; and the numbers `27-61` will cite.

Implements D-52 and D-56 item 1 (the completeness claim is derived, never a hand-listed product),
D-45 and D-49 (the corpus is built from outside the rule under test), D-30, and D-19.
</objective>

document, before anything is located"; it becomes "the body used for the prose checks, after the region
is located".

for goes to the failure arm" true inside the region as well as outside it.

once inside the parse entry point and only on the text used for the prose body. It goes red if a future
phase moves the strip back in front of the region location.

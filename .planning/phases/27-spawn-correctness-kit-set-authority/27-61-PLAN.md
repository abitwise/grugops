<objective>
Close the round: prove the round's own pins can fail, account for every item round 10 raised, and hold
the traceability record to its written rule.

Three obligations, none of them optional under this phase's recorded conventions.

**The mutation sweep.** This phase's terminal lesson list ends with *assert the verification harness's
own premise* — it produced a false result in six instances across four straight rounds. Every plan in
this round carried its own local mutation control; this task runs the consolidated sweep across all of
them at once, on the final build, so no pin is trusted merely because the plan that wrote it said so.

**The disposition register.** Round 10 raised TEN items. The register is this phase's durable answer
to *"what happened to everything the last round raised?"* — a finding that leaves a round without a
written disposition is indistinguishable, to a later reader, from a finding that was forgotten, and
this phase's own experience is that such an item returns one abstraction level down. Ten items in, ten
rows out, with the completeness asserted by count.

**The traceability hold.** D-58 item 4 is explicit: only a verification round may flip a row to
Complete on the strength of new work. KIT-03 and SPAWN-04 stay `[ ]` / Gaps Found even though this
round targeted exactly their defects — eleven rounds have paid for what a premature flip costs, and
commit `47d7820` already reverted this exact pair once. SPAWN-03 stays deferred with its owner named.

Purpose: the trace is the proof. A register with nine rows for ten items, or a row promoted by the
plan that wrote its fix, breaks the only mechanism this phase has for not repeating itself.

Output: a consolidated mutation sweep with two premise controls, a ten-row register with an asserted
count, the standing obligations re-measured or restated with owners, the round-wide probe equality in
one place, the requirements record asserted rather than edited, and D-63 recorded.

Implements D-58 (the traceability convention, all four of its clauses), the D-41 / D-47 / D-50 / D-53 /
D-56 posture (the carried findings close in the same round, none deferred) in its sixth application,
D-19, and this project's binding no-fabrication constraint.
</objective>

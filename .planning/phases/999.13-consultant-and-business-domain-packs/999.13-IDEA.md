# 999.13 — Consultant and business-domain packs (idea)

Captured 2026-11-16 by the human during `/gsd-execute-phase 42`, while deciding not to use third-party skills.

## The idea

- grugops needs a way to add business agents for different business types. They help the business analyst and answer business-related questions.
- The roles can go beyond software delivery. A game studio, for example, needs a game designer, an artist and a sound artist. These act as consultants.
- A consultant role could be created on demand for each consultancy purpose, to give better answers than a general agent.

## Shape discussed (not decided)

- **Advisory only.** A consultant answers the BA/PM and other roles. It writes no code, claims no tickets and owns no gate. This fits grugops's hard limits.
- **File-based pack.** A pack is a folder of consultant role and checklist markdown. The existing role guards admit it (`admitRole`, heading order, byte ceilings), and a named human ratifies it by typed name. There is no registry, no download and no runtime.
- **Quality comes mostly from curated domain content** (question banks, glossaries, checklists), not from the persona name.
- **Dynamic consultants.** The Orchestrator drafts a consultant from a template. Its output is marked unverified, because nothing enters the shared verified context unverified. A human can ratify a useful draft into a pack, so it is reused and improved.

## Constraints to settle first

- PROJECT.md Out of Scope says: "An agent marketplace beyond the single-plugin catalog — out of scope to avoid building a platform". A pack system needs a recorded decision that amends or narrows that line.
- The role byte budget (one corpus total of 67,383 B, D-1) covers kit roles. Decide whether pack roles count against it or have their own ceiling.
- Related: Phase 42 decided (2026-11-16) not to recommend third-party skills, for security, complexity, performance and licensing reasons. A pack must be authored or ratified content, not third-party code.

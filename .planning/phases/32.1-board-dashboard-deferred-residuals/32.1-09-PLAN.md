<objective>
Close WR-01 and F-20 (ledger row 206), the two items that live in the watch arm.

WR-01 is a contract drift: the normative contract states in bold that a directory the projector will
not watch carries no watch record, and a fix two rounds ago made that false for two of the three
refusal codes. The code's own comment argues the change correctly and even notes that the contract's
justification is true only of the containment code — but the contract was not moved, and this
repository's rule for its own contracts is that a newly admitted shape is recorded there first and
implemented afterwards. Nothing detects the drift either: the existing run-time contract-versus-code
equality is scoped to a different table, so the watch paragraph is kept in agreement only by eye,
which is the failure mode WR-01 was filed for one section over.

F-20 is the same arm's other half: the handle is opened on the resolved path but the liveness gate
that decides whether to open it still asks about the unresolved one. One containment decision,
consumed by two spellings.

Purpose: DASH-04 and DASH-05 rest on the watch loop doing what the contract says. A contract a reader
adjudicating a disagreement consults, which states the opposite of the code, is worse than no
contract.
Output: the replacement paragraph, the behaviour it describes, a second differently-shaped equality
instrument that detects the next drift, and one consumer moved onto the resolved spelling.
</objective>

## Decisions Made

- **CI_EXEMPT was frozen and routed through one accessor.** The census needed three members so task 2's deletion could clear the floor, and `CI_EXEMPT` was carrying the identical unguarded prototype lookup at three read sites. Freezing it made the three registers one declared form and applied the IN-03 remedy a second time rather than inventing a second spelling of "classification register" for the census to recognise.
- **The census recognises registers by the canonical form `const X = Object.freeze({ … })`.** A register spelled another way is not admitted by a second arm; it is a register this rule does not govern, and the boundary is stated in the banner.
- **The toolchain class was deleted rather than kept.** The plan carried an explicit guard: do not delete if the census would drop to one member or fewer. Measured, it drops from three to two, so the deletion proceeded and the fallback was not taken.
- **The IN-03 prototype-member case and its premise case were re-homed onto CI_EXEMPT.** The property did not belong to the deleted register; it belongs wherever a register's membership is a recorded decision.
- **Findings are asserted before moved numbers.** An earlier ordering red on the read-count pin for the F-19 plant; a bare count mismatch is the least informative way to learn that a second authority arrived, so the unguarded-read list is asserted first.

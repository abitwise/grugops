## Decisions Made

- **The second predicate is the runtime, not a second list.** A hand-written "is this a builtin" list beside `ALLOWED_BUILTIN_SPECIFIERS` would be the set-literal drift class this repository has already paid for, and it would not be independent of the list it sits beside. Asking `node:module` is a genuinely different authority.
- **Normalize before asking.** `node:sqlite` is a builtin only in its prefixed spelling, so `isNodeBuiltinIdentity` asks both spellings through `normalizeSpecifier` — the same helper the allow-list equality reduces through — rather than introducing a second notion of module identity.
- **The retired route is the `opaqueSpecifiers` one, not the `acquisitions` one.** `acquisitions` was already total for the dynamic-import question and it is the register whose emptiness is the write-detection mechanism; deleting the other way round would have moved the refusal from the mechanism to a specifier census.
- **`require(expr)` keeps its route.** `collectAcquisitions` decides `require` through its unresolved-callee arm rather than as a specifier slot, so deleting the whole route would have left that shape decided by a message about a callee name and by nothing about the specifier.
- **Row 208 gets ONE composed sentence.** The waive verb is one-shot and overwrites the reason cell; the row covers two structural halves and has one cell, so the halves are composed here rather than at call time.

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] The refusal sentence was being truncated by `briefly()`**

- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** The node-kind refusal was assembled as `briefly(\`${refusal} — ${text}\`)`, and `briefly` caps at 140 characters. The two longer kind names (`TemplateExpression`, `BinaryExpression`) pushed the sentence's tail past the cap, so the canonical-form clause was cut. Measured: `2 failed | 200 passed (202)`, with only the shortest kind passing. A refusal that says less the more unusual the shape it refuses is exactly backwards.
- **Fix:** `briefly` is applied to the SOURCE TEXT only — which is what its own docblock says it exists for ("a whole call or declaration can be long"). Applied to the literal-argument branch too.
- **Files modified:** `scripts/board-readonly.test.ts`
- **Verification:** `202 passed (202)`; the three post-change transcripts in `32.1-08-RED-baseline.txt` § 5.2 are from after the correction, and § 5.3 records the failure.

<objective>
Finish Gap B the way the gap-closure guidance requires: derive, do not hand-list. The fs census from plan
33.1-18 classifies every mutating fs call site on one axis, whether it is gated under DRY_RUN. That axis
proved `unmergeGemini` and the old empty-file remover DRY_RUN-safe and said nothing about whether they may
touch the file at all, which is how Gap B survived round 1. This plan adds the second axis, ownership, over
exactly the modules the uninstaller runs. Every delete or edit site must say what makes the thing it
touches grugops's to change, and the census checks the claim's gate in the syntax tree. This is gap-closure
round 2 of the 4-round cap (D-06).

Then extend the directory-aware DRY_RUN matrix to the new ownership paths (D-20's proof bar: dry-run-capable
and reversible), and publish Gap B's behaviour in install/README.md and CHANGELOG.md. Because plan 33.1-27
put every removal of a grugops skill or adapter file on a recorded content hash or link target, no caller
class in this census rests on a name alone. The census holds that property from here on.

Purpose: a future delete or edit on the uninstall path cannot land without an ownership class, and a claimed
gate cannot quietly move.
Output: the ownership axis, the named removal passes, flows 9 and 10, and the Gap B published text.
</objective>

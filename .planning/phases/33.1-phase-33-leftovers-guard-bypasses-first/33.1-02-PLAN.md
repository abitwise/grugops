fail-closed arm across every wrapper family. This is the fix for the coverage blindness that let a

fix stated as membership in the allow-list rather than one more refused shape (D-01), and it closes the
CR-02 positional forms because a double-quoted run holding an expansion is now opaque.

Purpose: put the canonical-form authority in place and prove CR-01 + CR-02 across the full family ×
arm matrix. Output: C1 in `scripts/checkpoints.ts`, a derived corpus, and a count-asserted sweep.
</objective>

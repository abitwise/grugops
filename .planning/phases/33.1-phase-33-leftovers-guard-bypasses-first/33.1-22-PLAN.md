where the state literals may be produced, and a type-checked census pins where a host-JSON field may be read
at all (brief 33.1-GAP-PLANNING-BRIEF.md §2.2, DC-1: the reader is the only place host fields are read), so a
future ad-hoc reader cannot slip past it. The behavioural half of the derivation is plans 33.1-23 (every
evidence field alone, plus the cross-row branch-policy rule, re-review CR-02) and 33.1-24 (fields in pairs).
Plan 33.1-25 writes the published text.

<objective>
Make claim-dropping mechanical. When a floor is lowered, the public sentence it backed is replaced in
place under its own anchor by generated disclosure text naming the checkpoint, its value and the
authorizing human; the registry row records the drop; and the anchor gate compares against the
generated text so prose and mechanism cannot drift.

Purpose: this is the payoff of the whole phase. A lowered floor that leaves an overstated claim
standing in the documentation is the failure mode the milestone named at kickoff, and only a
generated, gate-compared replacement closes it.

Output: a dropped status the registry parser accepts, an anchor gate that compares against generated
text, three anchored pointer lines, and the settings-file residual recorded in the register.
</objective>

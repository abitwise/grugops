<objective>
Derive the full checkpoint roster from the kit itself: tag the 38 workflow `## Stop conditions`
bullets that are genuine human stops, walk them with the tree's one section locator, and assert the
derived set against the exported roster in both directions.

Purpose: set-literal drift is this milestone's founding defect. A hand-listed checkpoint array beside
a separate default map is two declarations of one set. This plan makes the roster derivable and then
makes the derivation and the roster prove each other.

Output: a complete `Checkpoint` union whose defaults table is its only declaration, a derivation that
refuses a short or empty corpus, 38 tagged bullets across 19 workflow files, and a companion
disposition row for every frozen-section edit.
</objective>

<objective>
Close the process-side and portability-side items round 6 inherits, and pay down the debt round 5
measured and named: the §14 gate's missing signal arm (D-28's own recorded non-establishment, at the
one caller that can answer for it), the Windows leg of everything rounds 4 through 6 measured on
darwin only (`R-03`, `R-31-19-03`), the installer round-trip on a pre-existing host install (`R-04`),
the `check:diff-disposition` debt (35 findings owed by round 5's own plans), the unreconciled
harness-instance tally, and the `.temp/` residue predicate that cannot observe its own subject.

Two of these are corrections to what the record says, and both matter more than the work itself.

**`R-03` is not a missing CI job.** `.github/workflows/ci.yml` already runs a `windows-latest` leg
that installs, builds, typechecks and runs the whole excluded-e2e suite. Writing a plan that adds a
job which already exists would be a fabricated closure — exactly the thing this phase's requirement is
about. What is actually missing is that the Windows leg exercises NONE of the shapes rounds 4, 5 and 6
measured: the non-regular-file class behind CR-12 and CR-17, the spec-integrity runnable's exit-code
contract, and the degenerate-directory-identity platform behind `R-31-19-03`. So this plan adds
Windows-scoped STEPS to a job that exists, drives the portable shapes there, and records a loud named
skip for the shapes Windows cannot construct — `mkfifo` has no Windows equivalent this harness can
create, so the DIRECTORY is the portable non-regular-file shape and the FIFO is POSIX-only. `R-03`
then shrinks by exactly what was measured, and what remains is stated by name.

**`R-04` was never a human item.** It has been carried unchanged for five rounds as requiring a second
scratch repository with a prior install. That is a harness, not a human: a temporary home, an install
at the prior shape, a second install, an idempotence assertion, a materialization assertion for the
runnable Phase 31 added, and an uninstall that removes it. It is automated here.

The remaining three are bookkeeping the round-5 closing measurement raised and could not repair,
because a measurement plan that writes source has found a new defect. The debt is paid in the owning
plans' own disposition files — without moving the base commit or narrowing the corpus, both of which
the gate's own message names as clearing a finding by deleting its evidence. The instance tally
becomes one tracked list with the numbering read off it, because two plans of round 5 both claimed the
ninth instance and a prior SUMMARY is history that is never rewritten. And the residue predicate stops
being a command that cannot see its own subject.

Purpose: close the process-side holes and the measurement debt, so round 6's fix plans are measured
against a record that is not itself drifting.

Output: a signal arm in the §14 gate's exit-code branch with a test binding it; Windows-scoped CI steps
driving the portable shapes with a recorded skip list; an automated installer round-trip; three
disposition files paying round 5's debt; one tracked harness-instance list with a gate; a real residue
predicate; refreshed carry-forward records; and decision **D-32** appended to `31-CONTEXT.md`.

<objective>
Prove the three gaps are closed at the coordinates the verifier used, not at coordinates chosen
afterwards. This plan re-runs 31-VERIFICATION.md's three Behavioral Spot-Check commands verbatim
against the post-fix tree, records their outputs beside the originals, measures the whole tree green
at one commit, writes the reachability enumeration down as numbers, assembles the residual register,
and leaves a closure brief whose single question a named human answers at end-of-phase harvest before
the phase returns to verification.

Purpose: this project's standing doctrine is that a green suite is not proof for a safety predicate,
and Phase 31 is a live instance — 61 test files and 3264 tests were green while all three gaps were
open. The only thing that distinguishes this round from that one is a measurement taken at the
defect's own coordinates.

Output: `31-08-SUMMARY.md` carrying a three-row re-run table directly comparable to the verification's
own, a whole-tree green record at one commit, an enumeration table, a residual register, and a
closure brief ending on the one question a named human answers.
</objective>

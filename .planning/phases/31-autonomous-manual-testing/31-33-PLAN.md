<objective>
Establish D-31 (2)'s rule at the ENTRY of every route that writes a note and its GOV-02 ledger event,
and make a skipped note entry report the condition that is actually true.

Two Criticals, one module, one generator:

- **CR-22 (UATX-01)** — `promoteAdmitted`'s `destinationRoot = governanceRootOf(to)` derivation (line
  2493) sits BELOW the function's own human-stamp fall-through (lines 2416-2419), so an ordinary
  non-human-stamped note returns before reaching it; and `admitAndAppend` — named by hand in
  `agent-factory/workflows/18-context-compaction.md` as the other route that writes both — never
  received the derivation at all. `31-VERIFICATION.md` round 7 independently reproduced BOTH: a note
  and its own audit record landing in two different governed repositories.
- **CR-24 plus the Skipped-entries finding (UATX-01)** — `readRawNotesWithSkips` discards the
  `ReadPositionRefusal.condition` discriminant D-31 (3) created for exactly this caller and files
  three conditions under one arm; the test that claims to prove the arms distinct expects two of them
  to be the same arm and never compares the colliding pair.

Both fixes are structural in the same way: a property claimed of a function is installed at the
function's entry, and a property claimed of a set is asserted over a set DERIVED from the module's
own AST with its cardinality — not over the two or three positions a reproduction happened to walk.

Purpose: the shared verified context is this project's only memory between agents; a finding whose
audit record lives in another repository, and an unreadable note that points a human at the wrong
cause, are both records that cannot be trusted at the moment they matter.
Output: one derived owning-repository per action on every write-both route; a discriminant-named skip
arm with a bound arm set; a corrected workflow sentence; decision `D-34` recorded.
</objective>

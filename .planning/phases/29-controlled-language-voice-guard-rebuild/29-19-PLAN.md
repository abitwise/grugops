<objective>
Record the human override that closes LANG-08.

LANG-08 asks that byte ceilings be re-baselined exactly once at end of phase, every file at or under
its previous value, the delta recorded, and never raised mid-phase. Three of those four hold by
construction and by transcript: nothing was raised, nothing moved at all, and the delta is recorded in
two audit documents. The central action — re-baselining once — did not occur, and it did not occur
because a human was asked at plan 29-13's blocking checkpoint and chose `hold-rebaseline`, on the
stated ground that re-deriving the margin from today's smaller corpus would convert the headroom
Phases 13 to 27 absorbed into permanent new headroom.

The verification report treated that as a gap rather than silently accepting it, which was correct,
and it drafted the override that closes it, which is what this plan writes down. The user has decided
between the two remedies the report offered: record the override, do not apply the ratchet-down. So
this plan changes no ceiling, applies no computed value, and touches no source file.

The one thing it must not become is a paperwork exercise that quietly loosens something. The scope is
one frontmatter block, one counter, and a proof that the ceiling table did not move anywhere in this
round.

Purpose: close LANG-08 by acknowledgement, with the decision, its author, its time and its reason all
in the record.
Output: an override block in the verification report's frontmatter and a scoped diff proving no
ceiling moved.
</objective>

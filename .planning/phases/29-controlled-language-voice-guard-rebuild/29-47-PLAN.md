<objective>
Attack what this round built, on the axis this round introduced — then write down what was decided, what
was tried, and what remains.

Round 5's adversarial log has thirteen attempts and its own honest framing, and it still missed the
defect that mattered, because every attempt varied an INPUT and none varied the one degree of freedom the
round's own design decision had added. That is the transferable lesson and it is the whole reason for this
plan's first task: **name the degree of freedom the change introduces, then attack that.**

Deleting the conditional half of the comprehension group removes the verb axis and opens four others.
**Substring reach** — the two terms now match unconditionally, so they also match inside longer words and
inside text a reader would not call prose. **The region as the sole carve-out** — the exemption boundary is
now the only thing standing between a legal denial and a red, which raises the value of every boundary
attack. **Term membership** — with no marker list, the only thing still enumerated is the two-term set, so
a claim written with neither term passes, and that is the residual this gate has always had and must state
plainly rather than let a closure imply away. **The widened corpus** — a document entered the scan this
round, and whether the file-scoped exemption and the pins behave correctly over it is a question nobody has
asked.

And the deletion moves the carried residuals in both directions. A window residual about a co-occurrence
split across a hard wrap cannot reach a member that no longer co-occurs; a false-positive residual about a
table row or an HTML comment cannot trigger on one either; and the residual whose single live instance was
the ordinary-English occurrence plan 29-44 rephrased may have gone to zero. A roll-up copied from round 5
would be false in both directions, so every carried id is re-measured over the final tree.

Purpose: a green suite is not proof for a safety invariant in this repository. The credible artifact is a
written list of what was tried and what happened — including the attempts that worked — and a reconciliation
that makes a silent drop impossible.

Output: the adversarial pass with one plant per reset mirror; `docs/audit/29-round6-residuals.md`; the
twelve-row reconciliation of round 5's findings; and the sweep.
</objective>

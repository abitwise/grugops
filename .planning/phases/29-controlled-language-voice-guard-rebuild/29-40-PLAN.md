`generate-role-adapters.ts:258`.

non-mechanical step is the return shape — `Parsed<FrontmatterKeys>` is a discriminated result over a
`Map<string, string[]>`, so every call site gains a parse-failure arm and a multiplicity question the
private last-wins regex never had to answer.

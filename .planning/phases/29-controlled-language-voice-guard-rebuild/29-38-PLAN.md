<objective>
Close **WR-06** of `29-REVIEW.md` (round 4).

Plan 29-31 narrowed **two** profile rows to the literal heading `## Steps` and titled the new profile
section "The heading spelling `WP-11` and `WP-04` decide, and the floor beneath it", asserting of both that
they "name the literal heading `## Steps` … because that is the spelling the gate decides." The mechanism
built beside it holds `WP-11`'s two sentences only. `WP-04`'s row carries no constant in the gate, no
membership in the pin, and no assertion anywhere — a search for its id across the test modules returns two
comment lines and one unrelated case name.

So `WP-04` can drift back to "a bullet under a steps heading is procedural" — the level-agnostic wording
29-31 removed as **fail-open** — with no red, in the same document and the same commit shape the round's own
WR-05 was raised for. A published claim about two rules, held by an assertion about one, is the class this
work exists to remove.

Purpose: per D-11 and D-39 a guard is named for exactly what it decides, and that rule does not stop at the
gate's output line — a pin named for one rule while holding two is the same defect at the identifier. Per
D-33 the profile's own published claims are registered rather than trusted.

Output: WP-04's decidable half spelled once in the gate, emitted in the refusal that already cites it, held
by two new pin members, proven by two new mutations, and the pin renamed for the two rules it now holds.
</objective>

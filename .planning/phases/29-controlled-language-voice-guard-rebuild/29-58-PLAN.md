<objective>
Bring the kit's own prose to the same narrowness as the gate's published sentence, and pay the two-file
cost the freeze charges for it.

`agent-factory/writing-profile.md`'s honesty-floor section states in prose what plan 29-56 narrowed in
code: it says the guard holds all three prohibitions mechanically over the shipped artifact and the
user-facing documents. That is the same totality claim, in the one document the kit ships as its statement
of what it does and does not assert. Leaving it while the gate's own line is narrowed would leave the
disclaimer wider than the gate it cites — the defect one address to the left (D-55).

This edit is expensive by design. The sentence lives inside `C-28-042`, one of six registry-anchored
blocks that D-54 froze byte for byte, and since round 7 a line inside the exemption region is exempt only
while it also sits inside such a block. So the prose and its registry row are a single change in a single
commit, the block's frozen bytes move together, and every exemption pin the edit disturbs is re-derived
from the gate's own refusal text rather than recomputed by hand.

Purpose: the honesty floor is the one place a reader looks to find out what this project claims. It must
be the narrowest statement in the tree, not the widest.

Output: a narrowed frozen block with its row, a corrected registry `mechanism:` field, an honesty-floor
residual paragraph that names the hard-wrap axis by id, and every moved pin measured.
</objective>

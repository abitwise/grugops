<objective>
Close the two remaining in-scope findings, both of which are one authority disagreeing with another
about the same question.

IN-03 is a case-sensitivity split. `guard_voice` scans the clear-voice remainder for caveman lexicon
terms through `countLexiconTokens`, which matches case-insensitively. Before that scan it neutralizes
three verified clear-voice grug phrasings — the brand command, `grug voice` and `grug wink` — with
three case-SENSITIVE replacements. A sentence-initial `Grug voice` therefore survives neutralization
and reds the guard on correct text. Two halves of one identity question, two answers.

The dangerous half of this fix is that it WIDENS an exemption, and a `saw`-style sticky flag carrying
a safety exemption is a scope-widening smell this project has already recorded. So the fix ships with
its bound asserted from both sides: the exemption must swallow the brand phrasings in any case, and it
must NOT swallow a bare caveman token adjacent to them. A widening proven only in the permissive
direction is not proven.

WR-06's third locator is the banned-claim exemption region, decided by a bare heading-prefix scan
while the single fence toggle already exists and is already consumed by a sibling gate for exactly
this question. This direction fails closed — a truncated exemption region means more of the document is
checked — and it is fixed anyway, because the phase's whole thesis is that a second grammar over the
same bytes is a defect even when the two currently agree. Stating the asymmetry with 29-16's fail-open
sibling is part of the fix, not commentary on it.

Purpose: make the two remaining duplicated predicates read one authority each, with the widening
bounded and measured.
Output: a case-agreeing neutralizer with a two-sided scope control, a fence-aware exemption locator,
and proof that both gates' live output did not move.
</objective>

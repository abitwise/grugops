<objective>
Close the four findings that are all one shape: **a property asserted in the wrong place.**

`WR-06` — a case is named "the gate REFUSES a conditional member declared with an empty marker array."
Nothing in the gate refuses it. An empty marker array makes the same-line arm skip that member on every
line, forever, silently, while the PASS line keeps counting it among the pinned literals. The only thing
preventing it from shipping is a test-file expectation. A case whose name attributes a refusal to the gate
mis-states which artifact holds the invariant — and the gate's own AP-1 rule is that a PASS line must
never state a check it did not perform. The shape survives plan 29-44's deletion, because the discipline's
name is still a conditional member.

`WR-01` — the suppressed count's sub-breakdown reads six plus three plus two against a pin of twelve. The
measurement is six, two and four. The twelve was derived from a run and is right; the sub-breakdown was
typed, inside the paragraph that opens by saying the entrants were derived through the gate's own counter
rather than by reading the prose. The remedy is not a corrected sentence — a corrected sentence rots the
same way. It is to make the breakdown an **output of the traversal that already computes the total**.

`IN-01` — a magic negative in a field whose contract is "the measurement", meaning "not measured", in the
module that refuses that conflation everywhere else. `IN-03` — an equality that is genuinely non-circular
and no longer pins a number, with the loss unstated.

And `WR-04` — a control-byte assertion over two files, standing beside a repo-wide gate that already
decides the NUL half over every tracked path. The mechanism existed and covered 1588 files. What failed
was that nobody ran it before believing two greps. "We added an assertion" is a weaker and less
transferable conclusion than "run the repo-wide gate before believing a grep", and a weaker duplicate of
an existing authority is the D-24 shape this phase closed one module over.

Purpose: after plan 29-44 the prohibition is right. This plan makes the gate hold its own invariants and
publish its own numbers, so the next round has less prose to trust.

Output: the refusal inside the gate; the breakdown published by the run; the sentinel expressed in the
type; the growth made visible; and one authority for the control-byte class, with the boundary declared.
</objective>

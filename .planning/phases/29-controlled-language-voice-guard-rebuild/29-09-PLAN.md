<objective>
Apply the writing profile to the middle seven workflows — the UAT pack, backlog refinement, sprint
planning, the daily sweep, sprint review, retro, and release.

The mechanics are plan 29-08's, and the reason this is a separate plan is context rather than novelty:
seven files of sentence-by-sentence rewriting is already at the edge of what one agent's window holds
before the care degrades, and this phase's whole argument is that a careless rewrite of instruction
text is worse than no rewrite.

What is new here is the release workflow. Its `## Commit` and `## Stop conditions` sections hold the one
place in the kit where a deploy is withheld pending a named human, and both sections are frozen-set
sources. D-02 is why they are touched at all: safety sections receive the profile deliberately, one
judgement at a time, because leaving the kit's most load-bearing prose as its least controlled text
inverts LANG-02 exactly where two agents most need to reach the same act. D-04 is what makes that safe:
a changed clause there is refused unless its companion edit lands in the same commit. The shape may
conform; the permission may not move, and the clear-voice register stays.

Purpose: convert the middle third of the governed corpus, with the release workflow's safety text
treated as the most deliberate edit in the batch.
Output: seven conforming workflows, a per-file growth record, and a disposition file.
</objective>

For each file: strip bold labels from `## Steps` bullets and promote the verb to position zero; restate
determiner-subject and actor-subject bullets as instructions, since the actor is already established by

construction with whichever it means, and split chained imperatives into one instruction per sentence.

needed, record it as a disposition row with its reason and the corpus occurrence that justifies it, and
check plan 29-08's summary first in case the same call was already made.

reshaping them would be conforming to a rule the profile does not make.

three-way sentence partition into the disposition file: the clear-voice safety text that withholds a
production deploy pending a named human and must keep both its meaning and its register; the style-only
prose the profile governs; and anything already conforming. Then edit only the second set, and for any
change inside `## Commit` or `## Stop conditions`, name the companion edit in its disposition row and
confirm the disposition gate accepts it before continuing. A conforming shape is available; a softened
permission is not.

The sprint-planning and sprint-review workflows carry human-decision language of the same kind. Apply
the same partition-first treatment to them, at lower ceremony: identify the decision sentences, leave
their meaning fixed, and conform only their shape.

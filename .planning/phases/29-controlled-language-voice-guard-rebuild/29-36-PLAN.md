<objective>
Close **WR-01** and **WR-03** of `29-REVIEW.md` (round 4). Both are the same species: a predicate that
answers a WIDER question than the claim standing on it.

**WR-01.** `contractSitesIn` marks a call site GUARDED as soon as `guardRe(bound)` matches any line in the
window before the identifier's first use. It never checks that the comparison DOES anything. A block that
compares the index against negative one and then falls through, and only afterwards uses it as a slice
bound, is classified GUARDED — reproduced against the shipped classifier on a planted module. The
authority's own contract names defaulting-to-zero as the forbidden repair, and a comparison whose
consequent falls through IS defaulting to zero, spelled differently. The block's disclosed blind-spot
list names five shapes and not this one, while the case that consumes it is titled "the UNGUARDED set over
the live tree is EMPTY."

**WR-03.** `REACH.I5` uses `occurrencesOf`, which counts every line whose trimmed end equals the heading,
FENCED OR NOT. I5's loop body only reports when an EARLIER line is unfenced and equals the heading. So the
whole fenced-before arm of the duplicate axis counts toward the reach floor while being structurally
incapable of violating I5. Measured over the shipped 21600-cell corpus: reach floor 1800, cells that could
actually violate I5, 360. The case's own message — that I5 is exercised by 1800 cells and a zero here would
mean it had never been evaluated against a document that could break it — is untrue of 1440 of them. That
is a count standing in for the predicate it claims to measure, which is the exact defect WR-03 was raised
about one round earlier.

Purpose: per D-24 each question has one authority, and an authority is only worth its name if it answers
the question its consumers believe it answers. Both corrections are narrowings, and a narrowing has a
specific hazard this plan must discharge: it can quietly exclude the very evidence the claim rests on.

Output: a contract classifier that requires a guard to leave the scope, a reach predicate derived from the
invariant it measures, and both narrowings proven not to have dropped their own load-bearing cases.
</objective>

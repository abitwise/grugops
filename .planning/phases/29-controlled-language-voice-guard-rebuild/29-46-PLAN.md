<objective>
Close the three findings whose subject is **prose that contradicts the code beside it**.

`WR-03` — `scripts/generate-catalog.ts` states two different workflow cardinalities four lines apart. Four
statements say the directory keeps sixteen numbered files in one range; two statements added by plan 29-40
say nineteen, and nineteen is what the directory holds. Plan 29-40 wrote the correct count into the same
header block and left the contradicting one immediately below it — in a file whose own new comment says
that a comment outliving its construct is a defect, and so is one that resurrects it for a reader who
cannot tell prose from code. There is no behavioural effect: the file-matching regex is range-free and is
the actual contract. That is exactly why the number should not be there at all.

`IN-02` — a comment in the generator's test file claims the pinned line number is derived from the planted
bytes "so an unrelated edit cannot turn this pin into a nuisance red". The interpolation into the expected
message genuinely is derived. But the very next statement pins a fence line by equality, and eight lines
earlier another pins a closing offset by equality, and either reds on any edit that shifts a line. The two
premise assertions are RIGHT to exist — they prove the fixture is the fixture — and the sentence claiming
immunity is what is false.

`IN-04` — two audit headings now assert their own contradiction: one reads "MEASURED and OUT OF SCOPE —
CLOSED by plan 29-40", the other carries "OPENED THIS ROUND … CLOSED IN ROUND 5" in one cell. The
trail-not-tidy-state convention is right and the bodies should stay verbatim. A heading is different: it
is what a reader greps and what a table of contents renders, and "OUT OF SCOPE — CLOSED" reads as an
unresolved edit.

Purpose: this round's whole subject is a published scope wider than the mechanism behind it. Here it costs
no safety and every bit of trust, which is the currency this project's value proposition is denominated in.

Output: the numbers deleted from the prose that could only rot; a derived assertion so the class cannot
recur silently; the immunity sentence reworded to say which half is brittle and why that is correct; and
two headings that read as the trail they are.
</objective>

<objective>
Close the **LANG-07 failed truth** of `29-VERIFICATION.md` and **WR-08** of `29-REVIEW.md`. They name one
defect from two sides.

`sectionBody` in `scripts/generate-catalog.ts:86` and `scripts/generate-role-adapters.ts:126` is a THIRD,
independently maintained, verbatim-duplicated section-extent grammar. Its terminator is a whole-document
lookahead, so it is **fence-blind** — a level-two heading quoted inside a fenced example terminates the
capture early — and **level-blind** — the terminator names level two only, so a level-ONE heading after
the section does not close it and the capture runs on into the next top-level section. The level half is
byte-for-byte the defect `voice-model.ts`'s old section end was, the one that cost this phase plan 29-14
(the half-fix) and plan 29-20 (the correction).

It is not a reporting helper. Its output becomes the `description`, `Use when` and `One job` text in the
**generated Claude Code role adapters** and the rows of the kit catalogue, and `npm run freshness:adapters`
would then require a truncated or over-long capture to be committed.

Plan 29-29 measured this itself while deriving its own falsifiability floor and ESCALATED it rather than
absorbing it — the round behaving correctly under adversarial pressure. It does not change that LANG-07's
literal text is "never two grammars over the same bytes" and the tree currently ships three.

Purpose: per D-24 the answer is to DELETE the duplicate and consume the one authority, not to teach a
third copy two new rules. Widening a parser for the Nth time has never closed anything in this repository;
the moves that have worked are structural.

Output: both generators routed through `unfencedHeadingIndex` + `sectionEndIndex`, the generated artifacts
proven byte-identical, every affected pin re-derived rather than guessed, permanent behaviour cases on both
axes, and §9.3 amended to name what it omitted.
</objective>

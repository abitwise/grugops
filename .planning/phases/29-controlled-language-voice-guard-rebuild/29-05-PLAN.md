<objective>
Rewrite the first seven role files onto the canonical skeleton, and prove the per-role pipeline
end-to-end on the file the whole phase cites as its worked example.

`software-engineer.md` is the tracer. Measured, it restates its stop-clause four times — in
`## One job`, in the caveman block, in `## Responsibilities` item 4, and in `## Hard limits` — and the
implement, small-diff, tests, checks, docs content appears three times. D-19 fixes that by assigning
ownership rather than by trimming: one section owns the what, one owns identity, one owns the how, and
exactly one owns prohibitions. D-09 settles which section gives up its content — the caveman block
does, which is also what makes it safe for the blocks to become maximally grug while honouring the rule
that the joke never replaces the explanation. D-41 removes the last carve-out: the second-person copula
opener is illegal under the zero-copula rule in sixteen of seventeen blocks, and it gets rewritten
rather than exempted.

The binding constraint is bytes, and it is already spent. Eight of seventeen roles sit above their WARN
tier today. D-30 adds a sentence to all seventeen; D-19's delete frees only 27 to 106 bytes per role.
D-26 forbids raising the table. So D-37 makes remove-before-add a plan-level ordering rule with a
measured transcript at each step, and a role that still reds after every legal removal escalates to a
human rather than borrowing from the table.

D-47 is the hidden cost. `## One job`'s first sentence feeds three byte-gated generated artifacts
through a two-hop cascade — the adapters derive from the role, the skill twins derive from the
adapters, and the catalog derives from the role — so the generators run in one order, in the same
commit, and the freshness gates fire once on purpose. This is also the seam Phase 29.1 depends on:
doing it once, cleanly, here is what keeps that phase from regenerating everything twice.

Purpose: turn the phase's two role-prose guards from RED toward GREEN, one file at a time, with the
byte transcript on the record.
Output: seven rewritten roles, their regenerated adapters, a regenerated catalog, and a disposition
file.
</objective>
